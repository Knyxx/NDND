#### Motivations
- Seek traces of the [[Luminous Wardens|original caretakers]] and [[enclave]] it once served.
- Preserve and restore life where [[Chaos Energy]] has left ruin.
- Uncover the truth behind its [[RGB and the Ember Order|memory loss]] and purpose.

#### Personality
- Steadfast and dutiful, guided by an unwavering sense of responsibility.
- Gentle caretaker instincts, viewing living beings as a “garden to be tended.”
- Haunted by fragmented memories, prone to brief melancholy.
- Curious about the world’s changes, but anchored by ritual and routine.

#### Story
- Originally a simple automaton, [[Robust.Garden.Bot (RGB)|RGB]] tended the gardens of a small [[enclave]], nurturing crops and supporting the local temple of [[Selûne]]. Over time, the construct was upgraded by the [[Ember Order|FILENOTFOUND]], learning druidic magic and developing the rare ability to transform [[Chaos Energy]] into natural vitality.
- When word of an impending attack reached the [[enclave]], its people fled, leaving [[Robust.Garden.Bot (RGB)|RGB]] behind. The construct eventually fell into stasis, forgotten as time passed. The [[Ember Order|FILENOTFOUND]] later returned, recovered, and studied it, but unable to replicate the mysterious energy conversion, they stored [[Robust.Garden.Bot (RGB)|RGB]] in a remote village.
- Internal strife left the village vulnerable. It was destroyed and erased from memory, leaving [[Robust.Garden.Bot (RGB)|RGB]] buried in the ruins, a [[Weathered Chapel Token]] wedged into it's hand by a priest of [[Selûne]] in the infighting. Dormant again, it was reawakened by a surge from a nearby [[Dimensional Breach#Fracture|fracture]]. Though much of its memory had been lost, one singular purpose endured: to seek the [[Luminous Wardens|FILENOTFOUND]], the [[Ember Order|FILENOTFOUND]], and the [[enclave]] it had once served.
- For nearly two hundred years since, [[Robust.Garden.Bot (RGB)|RGB]] has wandered, pausing only briefly in scattered settlements, always drawn by faint echoes of purpose and fragments of its past. After the destruction of [[Elderrend]], it arrived at the [[Crossroads Encampment]], aiding in the settlement’s slow reconstruction.
- One night, while preparing a small plot for planting under the soft glow of the moon, a ripple of [[Chaos Energy]] surged through the settlement. True to its nature, [[Robust.Garden.Bot (RGB)|RGB]] stirred, moving to investigate...

Originally a simple automaton, [[Robust.Garden.Bot (RGB)|RGB]] tended the gardens of a small [[enclave]], nurturing crops and supporting the local temple of [[Selûne]]. Over time, the construct was upgraded by the [[Ember Order|FILENOTFOUND]], learning druidic magic and developing the rare ability to transform [[Chaos Energy|chaos energy]] into natural vitality.
When word of an impending attack reached the [[enclave]], its people fled, leaving [[Robust.Garden.Bot (RGB)|RGB]] behind. The construct eventually fell into stasis, forgotten as time passed. The [[Ember Order|FILENOTFOUND]] later returned, recovered, and studied it, but unable to replicate the mysterious energy conversion, they stored [[Robust.Garden.Bot (RGB)|RGB]] in a remote village.
Internal strife left the village vulnerable. It was destroyed and erased from memory, leaving [[Robust.Garden.Bot (RGB)|RGB]] buried in the ruins, a [[Weathered Chapel Token]] wedged into it's hand by a priest of [[Selûne]] in the infighting. Dormant again, it was reawakened by a surge from a nearby [[Dimensional Breach#Fracture|fracture]]. Though much of its memory had been lost, one singular purpose endured: to seek the [[Luminous Wardens|FILENOTFOUND]], the [[Ember Order|FILENOTFOUND]], and the [[enclave]] it had once served.
For nearly two hundred years since, [[Robust.Garden.Bot (RGB)|RGB]] has wandered, pausing only briefly in scattered settlements, always drawn by faint echoes of purpose and fragments of its past. After the destruction of [[Elderrend]], it arrived at the [[Crossroads Encampment]], aiding in the settlement’s slow reconstruction.
One night, while preparing a small plot for planting under the soft glow of the moon, a ripple of [[Chaos Energy|energy]] surged through the settlement. True to its nature, [[Robust.Garden.Bot (RGB)|RGB]] stirred, moving to investigate...
