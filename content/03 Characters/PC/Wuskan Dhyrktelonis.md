#### Story
- [[Wuskan Dhyrktelonis]] was navigating an unstable portal when the magic of his artefacts collided in violent discord. The surge tore a [[Dimensional Breach|rift]] between worlds, casting him into another realm. In the chaos, the clashing powers fused into a single, searing relic—the [[Echo of the Lost Horizon]]—which obliterated his other possessions but preserved him from destruction, as he slowly, over 30 minutes, went hurtling into the planet.
- He fell into [[Sundrbjǫrg]], a vast, simmering volcano. Its eruption scorched the land in fire and ash, taking countless lives and flinging [[Wuskan Dhyrktelonis|Wuskan]] northward, finally coming to rest deep in the jagged [[Varkundr]] Mountains. The [[Chaos Energy]] he carried seeped into the world, fracturing reality. [[Dimensional Breach|Rifts]] erupted, [[Warped|corrupting flora and fauna]] into grotesque forms, while [[Great Beasts|twisted creatures]] spilled through from other realms—an age later remembered as [[Svartdomr]].
- Trapped within the unyielding bubble wrought by these chaotic energies, [[Wuskan Dhyrktelonis|Wuskan]] lay suspended for centuries until it was found by the [[Ember Order]]. Having pieced together it's history though tales and legends, they toiled to unravel the sphere’s secrets, but their every effort failed. At last, they consigned him to storage in the [[Crossroads Encampment]], where he endured in silence—an untouched relic of ancient chaos as the generations passed.
- Then, one night, a [[Shimmer|rogue]] slipped into the warehouse, ignorant of the prize before her. With a careless touch, she disturbed the great sphere. It shifted—slowly at first, then with mounting, unstoppable momentum—rolling toward her...
