NPC | [[Goliath]]

Kaelen, a female [[Goliath]] of the [[Jade Peaks]], guided her people through the cloud-wreathed passes to safety during the [[War of Broken Peaks.]]

Using her knowledge of the highland mists, she led them past enemy forces and treacherous terrain. Those who tried to stop her were met with lethal force, ensuring the survival of all she protected.

For this, her people named her _Mist-Warden_.

She later aided [[Paladin King Ada|Ada]] in hunting [[Warped]] in the region, and eventually joined him on expeditions across other lands. Through these deeds, she became a trusted friend, valued ally, general, and was entrusted with the seal [[Fire Disk]].

Kaelen later settled with the [[Mistwalker Clan]].
