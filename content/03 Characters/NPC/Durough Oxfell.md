#### Story
- [[Durough Oxfell|Durough]] grew up under the long shadow of his family’s warrior heritage, carrying the weight of expectation and eager to prove himself worthy of his bloodline. When his grandfather, [[Gromash Oxfell|Gromash]], took the family from the [[Oxfell Clanhold|Clanhold]], Durough saw an opportunity to demonstrate his courage. The brutal death of his grandmother, [[Hedra Oxfell|Hedra]], slain protecting his baby brother, [[Gar-ruk Oxfell|Gar-ruk]], revealed the harsh consequences of failure.
- Shedding his [[Oxfell Clan Studded Leather|old leather armour]], which he felt merely let him “hide behind the clan,” Durough refused to leave his grandfather’s side, determined to see his family safe. Working with the [[mercenary group]], he quickly honed his skills, favouring strength and brutality, and became a trusted and capable member.
- When his grandfather left to scout ahead and never returned, Durough was struck with grief and driven to find answers. Over years of service with the mercenaries, he discovered their deeper purpose: they were part of the [[Ember Order]], sworn to protect the world from [[Chaos Energy]], the same force that had destroyed his old home and clan in [[Elderrend]]. Swearing himself to their cause, he committed fully to their mission.
- Success finally came when his investigations led him to reports of an old [[Orc]] prisoner in [[Skywatch Fortress]]. Planning an assault to rescue his grandfather, Durough sneaked into the fortress and encountered a woman drawing a weapon. Seeing his unbound grandfather in front of her, he struck immediately.
- Gromash, distraught, grabbed the woman’s necklace—the [[Fire Disk]]—and dragged Durough away as he tried to check on him. It became clear that the relic his grandfather sought to protect was the very item the Ember Order had tasked them to retrieve. Struggling to reconcile the destruction his order had caused, and seeing his grandfather opposed to those he had fought and bled alongside, Durough lashed out in frustration, striking Gromash before being forced away.
