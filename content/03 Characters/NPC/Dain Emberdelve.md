NPC | [[Dwarf]]

Dain, a [[Dwarf]] of the [[Kraghnok Mine]], left his post to join the armies of [[Paladin King Ada]] during [[Svartdomr]].

Relentlessly standing his ground side-by-side with [[Paladin King Ada|Ada]] during the early delves into [[Sundrbjǫrg]], he quickly earnt the title _Deep-Warden_, and was named general before the campaign was declared impossible to complete. 

Dain spent some time travelling to the deep places following [[Svartdomr]], returning occassionally to visit his friend [[Paladin King Ada|Ada]] until he was entombed, trusting Dain with [[Acid Disk]].

Dain eventually returned to his post in the [[Varkundr]] mountains where his decendents still reside.
