# Thoughts
- Stay close to 5e for clarity
- Keep complexity low
- Cut low-value admin; prioritize fun
- Make meaningful changes, then let them settle
- Don’t patch character flaws with rules
- Balance every boost
- House rules should benefit everyone, not force priorities

# Reasoning / Balance
- Global: All benefits are mirrored by NPCs.

## Combat
- ([[#Exhaustion]], [[#Overextension]])
- Goal: Enable more epic moments
- Balance: Strong buffs countered by severe risks (e.g. [[#Exhaustion]], instant death)
- Risks: Abuse of powerful abilities; reduced relevance of core skills and other PCs

## Crafting
- ([[#Foraging]], [[#Craft Consumables]], [[#Phial Recipes]], [[#Quick Phials]])
- Goal: Expand options for health, survivability, and playstyle
- Balance: Costs time and resources
- Risks: Reduced reliance on core skills and other PCs

#### Story so far . . .

Nearly a thousand years ago, the continent stood at the brink of annihilation during the [[Svartdomr]]. The [[Five Kings]] rose to meet the chaos, among them [[Paladin King Ada]], trained by the legendary [[Golden Ascendancy]], whose disciples mastered both sword and spirit. Over the centuries, the Ascendancy’s influence spread, yet some groups splintered, following personal beliefs, forging new paths, or seeking isolation to cultivate their own ways. One such offshoot became the [[Cloudrunner Clan]], devoted to [[Solaris]], who carves the path forward, and [[Lunaris]], who tends to those left behind. Though never numbering more than five hundred, they carved [[Skywatch Fortress]] into the cliffs of [[Mount Dawnveil]], overlooking the valley below and [[Cloudrest Village]], a steadfast sentinel of faith and protection.

In this fortress, [[Auros Cloudrunner]], a devoted Cloudrunner, wed [[Gaia]] of the [[Mistwalker Clan]], a smaller, younger clan living in the marshy wetlands of [[Silvermarsh]]. Their child, **Amos, the Deliverer**, was born into expectation and promise, trained in the sacred ways of both his parents. Swordplay, discipline, and reverence were instilled in him from the earliest years, the Cloudrunner ideals shaping his every step. Yet, when the time came to awaken his divine potential and light the [[Dawnbringer]] in the family ceremony, he failed.

Shamed by the failure, his father and the elders rejected him, renaming him **Zamos, the Wayward**. His mother, however, refused to abandon him. Openly, despite disapproval from the clan leadership, Gaia continued to train him—believing that strength of body and skill of arms must never be neglected, no matter the failings of the spirit.

Zamos threw himself into labour, becoming a blacksmith to serve the clan while cultivating his martial skill. In the forge’s heat, he met [[Gromash Oxfell]], an [[orc]] prisoner of war pressed into labour. Night after night, they played cards and exchanged stories. The orc’s tales of distant lands and battles became a window to the wider world, and over the years, [[Gromash Oxfell]] became a surrogate father figure where Zamos’ own had turned away. Inspired by his mother’s faith and [[Gromash Oxfell]]’s adventures, Zamos resolved to leave [[Skywatch]] and seek his own path.

Compassion compelled him to release [[Gromash Oxfell]], but as the orc fled Skywatch, Zamos heard a commotion and went to investigate. He arrived just in time to see [[Gaia]] fall, struck down amidst the chaos of [[Gromash Oxfell]]’s desperate escape. Rage and grief surged through him, but even as he longed to demand answers, a heavier weight settled on his heart: he blamed himself, believing that had he not freed [[Gromash Oxfell]], she would still be alive.

Filled with both sorrow and fury, he pursued the fleeing [[orc]], desperate to confront him, yet before he could reach him, [[Skywatch Fortress]] was attacked. Assailants poured in from hidden paths, their strike swift and overwhelming. In the chaos, Zamos attempted to question some of the attackers he recognized from Gromash’s stories but was **struck and thrown from the battlements**, losing consciousness. When he awoke, the fortress had **burned and collapsed**, his clan destroyed, and the banners of the Cloudrunners no longer stirred in the wind.

Shattered but unbroken, Zamos vowed that mercy must never be undisciplined, and that the innocent would never suffer for his choices again. He tracked and hunted those responsible for the massacre, holding to the hope that Gromash had played no part beyond his mother’s death. During his pursuit, he uncovered **documents, images, and records of his family and clan**, revealing deeper plots centered in the **Ada Region**.

His path eventually brought him to the **Encampment**, a settlement rising from the aftermath of recent catastrophe. Expecting ruin and lawlessness, he found **hope** instead. Survivors worked together to rebuild, erecting monuments to the fallen, sharing burdens, and restoring community. The sight reminded him of Cloudrest village, of what his people had once protected. Here, Zamos found purpose: he could honor the lost, defend the innocent, and continue his hunt for justice.

Now, he walks the delicate line between vengeance and guardianship. By day, he aids the Encampment, defending those who strive to move forward from devastation.
