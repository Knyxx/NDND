#### 1. Total XP of all monsters
Total_XP = sum(Number × XP for each monster)

#### 2. Count total monsters
Monster_Count = sum(Number for each monster)

#### 3. Determine multiplier

|Number of Monsters|Multiplier|
|---|---|
|1|1|
|2|1.5|
|3–6|2|
|7–10|2.5|
|11–14|3|
|15+|4|
#### 4. Adjusted XP for encounter
Adjusted_XP = Total_XP × Multiplier
