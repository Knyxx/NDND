#### Traits & Resistances  
- All: resist necrotic, cold; resist nonmagical B/P/S  
- Enforcers: Bound to Skythane (advantage vs. turn/banish within 30 ft.)  
- Skythane: immune to poison and multiple conditions (frightened, grappled, restrained, etc.)

#### Tactics
- Skythane opens with Phantom Strike into the backline, uses Haunting Strike early, and keeps moving with Spectral Dash. Focuses on strong PCs or healers.
- Enforcers pin down melee fighters, flank to give Skythane advantage, and use Necrotic Grasp to restrain ranged or casters.
- Enforcers shield Skythane if he weakens.
- Defeating Skythane releases the bound enforcers, who flee.

#### XP Calculation
- Skythane: 1,800 XP  
- 2× Enforcers: 900 XP  
- Total Monster XP = 2,700  
- Monsters = 3 → Multiplier = 2.0  
- Adjusted XP = 5,400 → Hard for 6 level 5 PCs
