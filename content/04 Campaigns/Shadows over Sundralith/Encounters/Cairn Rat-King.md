#### Setting
Inside an ancient burial cairn, stone slabs are cracked and dust drifts in the stale air. Bones lie scattered among rubble. Faint squeaks echo through the narrow corridors.

#### Tactics
- Four rats emerge first, attacking PC
- At turn 3 or if half die, rat king growls and emerges with other 2 rats
- Raticate flees when at 25% health, Rattata flee with him
- If Raticate dies, all remaining Rattata flee

#### EXP Calculation
- Total Monster XP: 700
	- Rattata = 250 (5 × 50)
	- Raticate = 450
- Number of monsters = 6 → Multiplier = 2.0  
- Adjusted XP: 1,400 → Easy/Medium for 4 level 6 PCs
