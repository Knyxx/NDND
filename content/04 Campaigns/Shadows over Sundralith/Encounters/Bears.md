Encounter | 6×L3 Medium

#### Setting
The PCs enter a large moss-covered cavern, the air damp and still. Three massive cave bears lie in the center, deeply asleep on a bed of bones and fur. Disturbing them — even accidentally — will trigger a deadly fight.

#### Enemies
|Enemy|Number|HP|AC|Attack|XP|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|Cave Bear (adult)|2|34|11|Bite +5 (1d8+4); Claw +5 (2d6+4); Topple: When hitting a target with Claw, the target must make a DC 13 Constitution save or fall prone|200|
|Cave Bear (young)|1|27|12|Bite +5 (1d6+3); Claw +5 (1d8+3)|150|

Optional Environmental Hazard: Slippery moss, loose rocks, low stalactites — Dexterity saving throws (DC 12) may be required to avoid falling prone or triggering rockfall.

#### Tactics
- If the party sneaks successfully (Stealth DC 15 to move silently), they can pass or attempt to calm the bears (Animal Handling DC 18 for adults, DC 15 for young).
- If the bears are awakened, all three attack immediately. The adults use Multiattack (Claw + Bite) targeting the closest or loudest PCs.
- The young bear focuses on isolated or flanking PCs, attempting to separate them from the group.
- Bears may drag PCs into hazards if they succeed on grapple checks (Athletics DC 14).
- If one adult drops below half HP, it may try to retreat while defending the young bear, drawing the fight toward stalagmites or slippery edges.

#### XP Calculation:
Total Monster XP = (2 × 200) + 150 = 550
Number of monsters = 3 → Multiplier = 2.5 (for very high risk, multiple strong creatures)
Adjusted XP: 550 × 2.5 = 1,375 XP → Very Hard for 6 level 3 PCs

Optional Twist:
If the PCs make too much noise or use fire/torches carelessly, a collapse of stalactites or falling rocks (DC 14 Dex save, 2d10 bludgeoning) can occur mid-combat, turning a very hard fight into a chaotic, deadly scene.
