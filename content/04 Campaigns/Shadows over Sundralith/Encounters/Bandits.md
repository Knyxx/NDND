Encounter | 6×L3 Easy

#### Setting
The party comes across three bandits searching ancient ruins, overturning stones and prying open shattered urns. They haven’t noticed the PCs yet.

#### Enemies
|Enemy|Number|HP|AC|Attack|XP|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|Bandit Scout|1|16|13|Shortbow +4 (1d6+2); Shortsword +4 (1d6+2)|100|
|Bandit Thug|2|32|11|Mace +4 (1d6+2); Light Crossbow +2 (1d8+1)|100|

#### Tactics
- The scout keeps watch from a fallen column, firing arrows at ranged.
- The thugs fight defensively among rubble, using broken walls for cover.
- If outnumbered or group badly hurt, the scout calls for retreat, shouting that “the boss’ll want to know someone else found the ruins!”

#### XP Calculation:
Total Monster XP = (1 × 100) + (2 × 100) = 300
Number of monsters = 3 → Multiplier = 2.0
Adjusted XP: 300 × 2 = 600 XP → Easy for 6 level 3 PCs
