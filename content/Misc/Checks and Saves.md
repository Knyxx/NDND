#### Advantage / Disadvantage
- **Advantage**: Roll 2d20, take the highest
- **Disadvantage**: Roll 2d20, take the lowest

#### Checks and Saves
- **Ability/Skill/Tool Check**: d20 + Ability Modifier + Proficiency (if trained) + Expertise (if applies) + Other Bonuses
- **Saving Throw**: d20 + Ability Modifier + Proficiency (if trained) + Other Bonuses
- **Passive Check**: 10 + Ability Modifier + Proficiency (if trained) + Expertise (if applies) + Other Bonuses
