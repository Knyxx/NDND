#### Weapon Properties
- **Ammunition** – Requires ammo to make a ranged attack (1 piece per attack). Drawing/loading ammo is part of the attack.
- **Finesse** – Use **STR or DEX** for attack and damage rolls (choose each attack).
- **Heavy** – If your relevant ability score is **under 13**, you have **Disadvantage** on attacks with it.
- **Light** – When you take the **Attack** action with a Light weapon, you can make **one extra attack** with a **different Light weapon** as a **Bonus Action**. Bonus attack damage adds **no ability modifier** (unless negative).
- **Loading** – You can fire only **one piece of ammunition per action/bonus action/reaction**, even if you have Extra Attack.
- **Range** – Weapons list **normal/long range**. Beyond normal range: **Disadvantage**. Beyond long range: **can’t attack**.
- **Thrown** – Can be thrown as a ranged attack using the weapon’s thrown range. Use the **same ability modifier** as the melee attack.
- **Reach** – Adds **+5 ft** to your reach (including opportunity attacks).
- **Two-Handed** – Requires **two hands** to attack.
- **Versatile** – Can be used **one-handed** or **two-handed** for a higher damage die.

#### Weapon Masteries
- **Cleave** – On a hit with a melee weapon, make a second attack (same weapon) against a different creature within 5 ft of the original target. No ability mod to the second attack’s damage unless negative.
- **Graze** – If your attack misses, you still deal damage equal to your ability modifier instead.
- **Nick** – When making the extra attack from Light, you can make it as part of your Attack action instead of as a bonus action.
- **Push** – On a hit, push a Large or smaller creature up to 10 ft away.
- **Sap** – On a hit, the creature has Disadvantage on its next attack roll before your next turn.
- **Slow** – On a hit that deals damage, reduce the target’s Speed by 10 ft until the start of your next turn.
- **Topple** – On a hit, force the target to succeed on a Con save (DC = 8 + prof + ability mod) or be Prone.
- **Vex** – On a hit that deals damage, you gain Advantage on your next attack roll against that creature before the end of your next turn.
