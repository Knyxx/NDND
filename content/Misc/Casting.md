#### Spellcasting Focus
- A musical instrument, holy symbol, druidic focus or arcane focus.

#### Casting in Armour
- Must be proficient in armour type worn.

#### Targets
- Clear path to target - Cannot be behind total cover. For area of effect spells clear path must be to point of origin.
- Targeting yourself - If spell is target of choice, it can be caster, unless specified hostile or not caster (see spell description). Area of effect spells can also target caster.
- Awareness of being targeted - Target doesn’t know it was targeted by a spell, unless spell’s description says otherwise or the spell has perceptible effect.
- Invalid targets - Nothing happens, but spell slot is still expended.

#### Casting Time
- Only one spell with a spell slot per turn. On longer casting time the caster must take the Magic action and must maintain Concentration. If Concentration is broken, no spell slot is expended.
- Rituals - Take 10 minutes longer to cast.

#### Components
- Verbal (V) - Caster gagged or in area of silence can not cast a spell with verbal component.
- Somatic (S) - Caster must have one free hand to perform gestures for somatic component.
- Material (M) - Caster must have specific material. If material is not consumed and has no cost, component pouch or spellcasting focus can be used instead. Caster must have one hand free to access component, which can be the same hand as used for the somatic component.

#### Concentration
- If concentration is lost, spell ends.
- End concentration at any time, no action required.
- Casting another concentration spell, taking damage (DC = 10 or 1/2 damage amount rounded down, whichever is higher), being incapacitated or killed ends concentration.

#### Combining Magical Effects
- Effects of different spells add together and duration overlaps. Effects of same spells do not combine, highest bonus applies while durations overlap.
