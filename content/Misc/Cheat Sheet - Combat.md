### Your Turn = 3.5 Things
1. Action
2. Move
3. Bonus Action (if you have one)
4. Reaction (once per round, not on your turn)

### Actions
Unless specified, pick one.
- Attack – hit something
- Cast Spell – 1 action spells
- Dash – move again
- Disengage – no opportunity attacks
- Dodge – harder to hit
- Help – give ally advantage
- Hide – stealth
- Ready – act later (uses reaction)

### Bonus Action
Only if you have one:
- Off-hand attack
- Class ability
- Some spells

### Movement
- Move up to speed
- Split it anytime

### Reaction (1/round)
- Opportunity Attack (enemy leaves reach)
- Spells/abilities (Shield, etc.)

### Advantage
- Roll 2 dice:
	- Advantage = Use higher
	- Disadvantage = User lower

### Default Turn (if stuck)
- Move → Attack → Bonus (if any)
