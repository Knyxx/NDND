#### Your Turn = 3.5 Things
- Move
- Action
- Bonus Action (_if you have one_)
- Reaction (_once per round, often not on your turn_)

#### Actions (Pick 1)
- **Attack** – make a weapon/unarmed attack
- **Dash** – move up to your speed again
- **Disengage** – no opportunity attacks from your movement this turn
- **Dodge** – attacks against you have disadvantage _(if you can see the attacker)_
- **Grapple** – grab a creature to stop them moving
- **Help** – give an ally advantage on their next check/attack
- **Hide** – attempt to become unseen _(Stealth check)_
- **Improvise** – do something not covered by a standard action
- **Influence** – persuade, intimidate, deceive, or negotiate
- **Magic** – cast a spell or use a magical feature
- **Ready** – set a trigger to act later _(uses your Reaction)_
- **Search** – look for hidden creatures/objects (Perception/Investigation)
- **Shove** – push a creature away or knock them prone
- **Study** – examine a creature/object for clues or weaknesses
- **Utilize** – use an item, tool, or environment feature
**Or anything you can clearly describe to the DM.**

#### Bonus Action (Only if something gives you one)
- **Off-hand attack** _(two-weapon fighting)_
- **Class features** _(rage, cunning action, etc.)_
- **Spells** _(misty step, healing word, etc.)_

#### Movement
- Move up to your **Speed**
- You can **split movement** before/after actions
- Difficult terrain costs **double movement**
- Leaving an enemy’s reach may trigger an **Opportunity Attack**

#### Reaction (1 per round)
- **Opportunity Attack** – when a creature you can see **leaves your reach**.
	- Make **one melee attack** against it.
	- Doesn’t trigger if it **Disengages**.
	- Doesn’t trigger if it’s **teleported** or forced-moved _(pushed/pulled)_.
- **Spells/abilities** – _Shield, Counterspell, Absorb Elements, etc._
- **Readied Action** – if your trigger happens.

#### If You’re Stuck (Good Default Turns)
- Move > Attack > Bonus Action (_if any_)
- Move > Help the strongest ally
- Move into cover > Dodge
- Disengage > Move away
- Dash > reposition / reach objective
