- **Blinded**: can’t see, auto-fail sight checks, attacks disadvantage, attackers advantage.
- **Charmed**: can’t target charmer with attacks/harmful effects, charmer has advantage on social checks vs you.
- **Deafened**: can’t hear, auto-fail hearing checks.
- **Frightened**: disadvantage on checks/attacks while source in sight, can’t willingly move closer to source.
- **Grappled**: speed 0, disadvantage any but grappler.
- **Incapacitated**: no action/bonus/reaction, concentration broken, can't speak, initiative roll disadvantage.
- **Invisible** (per enemy): can’t be seen, attacks advantage, attackers disadvantage, initiative advantage.
- **Paralyzed**: incapacitated, can’t move/speak, auto-fail STR/DEX saves, attackers advantage, hits on your from within 5 ft auto-crit.
- **Petrified**: turned to solid substance, weight increase, cease aging, incapacitated, speed 0, attackers advantage, auto-fail STR/DEX saves, all damage types resistance, poisoned immunity
- **Poisoned**: disadvantage on attacks and ability checks
- **Prone**: attackers advantage within 5 ft, ranged attackers disadvantage, stand costs half movement
- **Restrained**: speed 0, attacks disadvantage, attackers advantage, disadvantage on DEX saves
- **Stunned**: incapacitated, speed 0, auto-fail STR/DEX saves, attackers advantage
- **Unconscious**: incapacitated, prone, drop held items, speed 0, attackers advantage, auto fail STR/DEX saves, hits on your from within 5 ft auto-crit, unaware of surroundings
