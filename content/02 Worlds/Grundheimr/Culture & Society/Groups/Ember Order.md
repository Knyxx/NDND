Society | Order

Founded by the descendants of [[Dain Emberdelve]], this ancient brotherhood is sworn to guard the world from [[Chaos Energy]], maintaining an unbroken watch over [[Acid Disk]].

Their emblem—a flame entwined with a rune of warding—marks eternal vigilance.

Members bear _metal-names_, denoting both rank and duty:
1. Adamant — Grandmaster, unbreakable protector and keeper of oaths
2. Mithril — Commanders, rare champions of endurance and divine favour
3. Gold — Loremasters, priests, and advisors guiding wisdom and prosperity
4. Silver — Seers, ritualists, and wardens against corruption
5. Steel — Captains and battle-forged leaders
	1. [[Durough Oxfell]]
6. Iron — Veteran warriors and front-line defenders
7. Bronze — Keepers of tradition, historians, and chroniclers
	1. [[Tor Emberdelve]]
8. Copper — Craftsmen, growers, and logistical sustainers
9. Tin — Scouts, messengers, and adaptable agents
	1. [[Askali]]
10. Lead — Warders of secrets, burden-bearers, and hidden guardians
11. Obsidian — Outcasts and shadow operatives, feared and distrusted

#### History
In the past have had differing opinions, some followed opposing beliefs, such as [[Selûne]] and [[Shar]]. 

Following significant loss, this was decided to be counter productive and avoided (see. [[Robust.Garden.Bot (RGB)|RGB]]).
