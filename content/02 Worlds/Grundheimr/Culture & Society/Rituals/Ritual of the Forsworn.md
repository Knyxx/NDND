#### Overview

The [[Restless#Forsworn|Forsworn]] challenge those who encroach on their domain—[[Player Character|PC]] or [[Non-Playable Characters|NPC]] alike.
The [[RItual|ritual]] has three steps. The spirit may prompt if the challenged are not forthcoming; failure may impose penalties.  
Total successes and prompts determine the final outcome when a request is made.

###### Step 1 – Truth of Self
- Action: Challenged speak name/purpose.
- Check: Persuasion (CHA) DC 13 or Deception (CHA) DC 16.
- Prompt: The spirit calls to the challenged, requesting their true intent.
- Success: The spirit recognises the challenged.
- Fail: The spirit is not convinced; DCs for subsequent steps increase by 2.

###### Step 2 – Respect of Domain
- Action: Kneel, hand on the earth or guarded focus.
- Check: Strength DC 13 or Wisdom DC 13
- Success: The spirit acknowledges your respect.
- Prompt: The spirit interacts with the area, gesturing or speaking.
- Fail: The spirit is angered by disrespect; the challenged takes 1d6 psychic damage.

###### Step 3 – Offering
- Action: Present food, drink, or symbolic sacrifice.
- Check and gift: 1d6 and Constitution DC 11 or food/drink and Religion (INT) DC 13.
- Success: The offering is accepted.
- Prompt: The spirit reaches toward the challenged or speaks of hunger or thirst.
- Fail: The spirit is angered by refusal; the challenged takes 1d6 psychic damage.

###### Step 4 – Request
- Action: Challenged make request of the spirit.
- Rewards:
	- DM considers request.
	- Successes without prompt:
		- 1: Advantage on skill checks for 1 hour.
		- 2: Advantage on attack rolls for 1 hour.
		- 3: +1 AC for 1 hour.

#### “The Spirit’s Steps”

Say your name, speak it true,
The spirits whisper back to you.

Kneel to stone, hand on ground,
The spirits watch, they circle ‘round.

Give a gift, small and sweet,
The spirits stir, they long to eat.

Ask your wish, soft and low,
Now awakened, they must know.

Step by step, they guard their land,
Step by step… they always stand.
