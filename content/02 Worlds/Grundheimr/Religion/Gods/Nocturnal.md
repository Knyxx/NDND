Religion | DaedricPrince

A [[Daedric Pantheon]].

Daedric Prince whose sphere is the night, darkness and mystery.

She is also known as the Night Mistress and Lady Luck.

Turned [[Shimmer]], a mischievous magpie, into an [[Elf]], gifting her the cursed [[Nightingale’s Pin]] and sending her out to retrieve gems
