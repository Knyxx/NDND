Location | Catacomb

Gravedeep (_GRAYV-deep_) - "Deep Burial"

#### Overview
[[Gravedeep]] is the vast catacomb network beneath [[Stonehowe]], first carved as communal burial halls for the ancestors. After the cataclysm of [[Svartdómr]] fractured the land, the caverns filled with the [[Restless]], until the [[Ashwardens]] reclaimed and sanctified them.

#### Structure & Features
- Labyrinthine tunnels and chambers, many warded and reinforced by the [[Ashwardens]].
- Sacred halls and niches holding the honoured dead.
- Fissures and hidden caverns where [[Restless]] spirits may rise, demanding constant vigilance.

#### Significance
[[Gravedeep]] is both revered and feared — a sanctuary for the dead and a crucible for the living. It serves as a burial ground, a site of ancestral communion, and a proving ground where the [[Ashwardens]] hone their craft against wrathful spirits. The people of [[Moundstead]] honour it with prayers and offerings, acknowledging Gravedeep as the heart of their heritage and the threshold to the underworld.
