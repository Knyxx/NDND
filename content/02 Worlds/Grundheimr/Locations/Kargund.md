Location | Sea

Kargund (_KAR-gund_) – “Tormented Sea”

Primordial ocean of chaos in Sundralithic lore, said to be the origin of storming energies and drifting stone fragments that shaped [[Grundheimr]] and the surrounding lands.

#### Overview
Vast, turbulent waters; conceptual rather than physically navigable. Mythically surrounds early Sundralithic lands, including [[Grundheimr]].

#### Environment & Hazards
- Storm-lashed waves, chaotic currents carrying fragments of shattered realms
- Extreme storms, unpredictable currents, and areas infused with lingering chaotic energy

#### People & Culture
- Revered and feared by the [[Sundralithic]] peoples as a primordial force
- Rituals and offerings to honor or appease the chaotic energies
- Old Sundralithic used in religious and scholarly texts
- Cultural influence on magic, runic lore, and cosmology

#### Points of Interest & Legacy
- Currents said to carry fragments of shattered realms
- Whispered locations of dormant energies older than the continents themselves
- Considered the primordial source of chaos from which [[Grundheimr]] and other lands arose
- Largely inaccessible and untouchable by mortals; serves as a conceptual anchor for world stability
