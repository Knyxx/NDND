Karnvald (_KARN-vald_) — “Realm of Karn”  

Named for the legendary smith [[Karn]], founder of [[Karnvok Mine]] and [[Karnhavn]].

#### Geography & Environment
- Rugged mountains, highlands, and scarred valleys of former ore veins  
- Harsh winters, brief summers; snowstorms, avalanches, rockslides  
- Borders: N – [[Adaheimr]] | NE – [[Jade Peaks]] | E –  | S –  | W – 
- Hazards: Collapsed tunnels, unstable cliffs, blizzards, rogue scavengers
- Resources: Iron ore, scattered minerals, timber, occasional magical rune-stones

#### People & Culture
- [[Human]], [[Dwarf]], [[Goliath]], [[Orc]]  
- Small hamlets; clan councils, scavenger clans, law by elders  
- Northern Common & Old Sundralithic  
- Traditions: Rune inscriptions for miners, seasonal safety festivals, ancestral worship  
- Trade: Salvaged metals, timber, carved runes  
- Threats: Frost wolves, ice trolls, cave-ins, rogue miners

#### Points of Interest
- [[Karnvok Mine]] – legendary veins, sealed and dangerous  
- [[Karnhavn]] – ruined capital, partially buried  

#### History
- Mine discovered by [[Karn]]; golden age of metalworking  
- [[Svartdomr]] – destroyed mine and [[Karnhavn]]; thousands died  
- Survivors scattered; some moved to [[Adaheimr]] or coasts  
- Legacy preserved in rune-stones and mining lore
