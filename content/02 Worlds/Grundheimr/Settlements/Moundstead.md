Moundstead (_MOWND-stead_) - “Settlement at the Mound”

#### Overview
Moundstead is a small village nestled beneath [[Stonehowe]], the Great Cairn. Its people farm, trade, and maintain rituals honouring the dead. The village exists in close partnership with the [[Ashwardens]], who protect the [[Gravedeep]] beneath the cairn and ensure the [[Restless]] dead do not rise.

#### Culture & Society
Villagers regularly make offerings of food, coin, or crafted items at Stonehowe.
The village acts as a support hub for the Askwards, providing shelter, supplies, and recruits.
Life in Moundstead balances daily work, family, and the solemn duty of remembering ancestors.
Relation to Stonehowe and the Ashwardens
The village maintains the original name of the cairn, Stonehowe, in rituals and prayers.
Ashwardens maintain a presence in the village as teachers, recruiters, and protectors.
The village thrives under their guardianship, while also participating in the sacred traditions of the cairn.
