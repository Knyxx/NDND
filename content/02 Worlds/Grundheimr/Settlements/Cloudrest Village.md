Settlement | Village

#### Type / Environment
- Mountain valley village surrounded by bamboo groves and rice terraces
- Natural environment with minor magical undertones linked to guardian presence

#### Location / Borders
- Situated in the [[Jade Peaks]] mountain range
- Across the mist-filled valley from the slopes of [[Mount Dawnveil]]
- Nearby landmarks: [[Skywatch Fortress]] (ruined), bamboo forests, terraces, rocky goat paths

#### Climate / Weather
- Misty, temperate mountain climate with frequent fog and light rains
- Warm, fertile summers for rice cultivation; cold, windy winters
- Occasional storms rolling down the peaks

#### Inhabitants
- Races: Humans predominantly
- Factions: Formerly influenced by the [[Cloudrunner Clan]]
- Monsters: Occasional mountain predators, roaming beasts in the higher slopes
- Notable NPCs:
    - Elders of Cloudrest, keepers of oral history
    - Survivors of the [[Cloudrunner Clan]]

#### Culture / Society
- Languages: Common tongue of the region
- Traditions: Lantern offerings to the guardians, harvest festivals, communal feasts, storytelling around fires
- Government: Village council led by elders; historically under protection of the Cloudrunners
- Trade: Rice, goat products, bamboo crafts; small trade with neighbouring valleys
- Religion: Veneration of [[Solaris and Lunaris]] as guardian spirits, ancestor worship tied to the Cloudrunners

#### Resources / Economy
- Natural Resources: Bamboo, rice terraces, goats, freshwater streams
- Trade Goods: Woven bamboo, rice, cheese, herbal medicines
- Magical Resources: Residual protective aura from the Cloudrunners and [[Skywatch Fortress]]

#### Hazards / Threats
- Environmental: Landslides, storms, river floods
- Political: Vulnerable without the Cloudrunners’ protection
- Monstrous: Mountain predators, beasts from higher altitudes
- Curse: The loss of [[Skywatch Fortress]] leaves lingering spiritual unease among villagers

#### Points of Interest / Landmarks
- [[Skywatch Fortress]] (ruined) perched on [[Mount Dawnveil]]
- Bamboo groves and rice terraces of Cloudrest
- Rocky mountain paths and goat herding trails

#### History / Lore
- Cloudrunners once defended the valley, integrating protection into daily life
- Tales of paladins descending to aid villagers, priests praying beside farmers during famine
- [[Skywatch Fortress]] destroyed in a catastrophic fire, severing the valley’s spiritual and protective bond
- Festivals and offerings once honored guardians who actively walked among the people

#### Story Hooks / Adventure Seeds
- Investigate the cause of Skywatch Fortress’s destruction—was it sabotage, a beast, or dark magic?
- Recover lost relics of the Cloudrunners scattered across the peaks
- Protect the village from rising threats without the fortress’s guardianship
- Discover whether residual magic from the Cloudrunners can still be awakened
- Aid villagers in preserving culture and morale after such profound loss
