Event | Agreement | [[_timeline#6 AD|6 AD]]

Completed atop [[The Oathmount]], the [[Adaheimr Treaty]] was the foundational accord established by [[Paladin King Ada]] after the end of [[Svartdomr]].

Brokered by Ada, one of the [[Five Kings]], the pact brought rival clans and towns together under a shared vow of peace and cooperation. It marked the start of regional reconstruction and formally named the land in honour of Ada’s efforts. Remembered as the cornerstone of [[Adaheimr]]’s identity, the treaty stands as a lasting symbol of unity, renewal, and the transition from war to peace.
