
##### Step 1: Journey to the Ruins

Environment: Forested foothills leading north.
Skill Checks / DCs:
- Survival DC 14: Track faint footprints, signs of fight.
- Perception DC 13: Spot wildlife or abandoned gear.
- Optional: minor hazards (fallen branches, slippery rocks).


##### Step 2: Bandit Camp

Location: Hidden glade/cliffside clearing.
Enemies:
NPC	AC	HP	Attack
Bandit (x4)	12	11	Shortsword +3 (1d6+1)
Bandit Leader	15	27	Shortsword +4 (1d6+2), Light Crossbow +3 (1d8+1)

Camp Layout:

```
           N
         Forest Edge
   [Tent B1]   [Tent B2]
       |           |
       |           |
        (F) Firepit
       / \       / \
  [C1]       [C2] Crates
          Path -> Ruins
```

Skill Checks / DCs:
- Perception DC 14: Spot camp before entering.
- Stealth DC 13: Approach undetected.
- Investigation DC 15: Find note revealing cave location.
- Insight DC 14: Realize bandits act for someone else.
- Intimidation DC 15 / Deception DC 16: Negotiate or extract info.

Loot / Clues:
- Note: “Capture the archaeologist and bring him to the cave. Paid in full by our employer. Leave no trace.”


##### Step 3: Cave Rescue

Cave Layout:

```
           Cave Entrance (E)
                 |
             [Snare S1]
                 |
   -------------------------
  /                         \
 [Bandit 1]             [Bandit 2]
      |                       |
   Restraints               Restraints
       \                     /
        \      [Torch]       /
         \                  /
          \ Loose Rocks Trap
           \
            Archaeologist

```

Traps / Hazards:
Hazard	DC	Effect
Snare / tripwire (S1)	13 Perception to spot, 12 Strength to free	Entangles a party member, alerts bandits.
Loose rocks	14 Dex save	1d6 falling damage
Torch bracket	14 Investigation / 15 Arcana	Minor trap if disturbed; can reveal hidden journal piece

Enemies:
- Bandits guarding archaeologist (stats as above).

Skill Checks / Options:
- Stealth DC 14: Avoid traps or bandits.
- Persuasion DC 15: Convince bandits to surrender.
- Investigation DC 14: Search for journal fragments and trap detection.
- Arcana DC 15: Neutralize minor magical traps.

Archaeologist NPC Traits:
- Elderly, sharp-minded, slightly frazzled.
- Motivated by clues about grandfather.
- Offers reconstructed journal info and historical knowledge as reward.


##### Step 4: Torn Journal Puzzle

Fragment Locations & Checks:

Fragment	Location	DC	Notes
F1	R1 (rubble outside cave)	Investigation 14	Partial notes on grandfather’s journey.
F2	R2 (collapsed wall near cave)	Investigation 14	Sketch of ruins layout.
F3	Bandit camp	Investigation 15	Torn piece left behind after capture.
F4	Cave floor (loose rocks)	Investigation 14 / Arcana 15	Arcane symbol hint.
F5	Torch bracket	Investigation 14 / Arcana 15	Minor trap; hints at hidden locations.

Puzzle Mechanics:
- Piece fragments together to reconstruct grandfather’s research.
- Arcana or History DC 15: Interpret cryptic notes or symbols.
- Insight / Intelligence DC 14: Understand context and implications.


##### Step 5: Return & Reward

- Deliver journal to archaeologist.
- Rewards:
	- Gold or items from archaeologist.
	- Reputation with Thyla’s organization.
	- Knowledge of lost history and clues for future quests.
- Optional twist: Mysterious benefactor may appear later, providing hooks for continuation.



##### Finding the Journal
###### Torn Journal Piece 1

*“Fire and frost, storms and bitter powers roamed the world. Folk trembled; even the stones shook beneath their feet.”*
*“Ada forged a Shield to guard the lands. No mortal could hold such power. He broke it into fragments, each entrusted to a guardian. Each bore its own force: some burned, some froze, some roared like storms, some healed — though all could wound if mishandled.”*

Margin note by Thorin:
Why does this feel urgent? The Great Calamity alone cannot explain it… The guardians, the fragments… something darker lingers.


*“Keep the pieces apart. If they meet, the Shield will break, and chaos shall return.”*

Margin note by Thorin:
Chaos return? Folk superstition, perhaps… but Grandfather never warned lightly. Where did you vanish to?


###### Torn Journal Piece 2

*“ᚨᛞᚨ (Ada)”*
*Ada’s rune! I found it!*

Margin note by Thorin:
Found what? Ada himself? Gone for ages… Why didn’t you come back?!


*I saw him again today. I knew I recognized him, and that he was watching my work too closely.*
*Tomorrow I’ll take what I can, grab what I need from the ruin, and leave before anyone notices.*

Margin note by Thorin:
What could matter more than us, old man? What were you hiding?

###### Torn Journal Piece 3

*Found another descendant. Their family had kept the old stories in stone, almost perfect!*
*I’ve translated what they had of ‘Ada’s Shield’. One line haunts me — is it never-ending?*
*“Touch not the Shield unless the fragments are kept apart, or all the world shall pay the price.”*

Margin note by Thorin:
Warnings piled upon warnings… The fragments alone aren’t the full threat. The Shield itself—another piece, another danger. Everything hinges on keeping them apart.

Doodles: ᚨᛞᚨᛋᚺᛁᛖᛚᛞ rune surrounded by four symbols, arrows linking warning lines to sketches.


*They found me. I was foolish to think I could escape.*
*I’m sorry, Thorin. I cannot keep that promise. I must keep it safe.*

Margin note by Thorin:
Keep what safe? What could matter more than kin? What became of you, old man?!

Marginal scribbles: dagger symbols, exclamation marks, arrows pointing to rune sketches of the Shield and fragments.
