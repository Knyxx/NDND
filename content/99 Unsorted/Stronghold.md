Reference

A fortified residence held by a single ruler, noble, or military leader, symbolizing authority, power, and strategic control. Serves as a defensive bastion, administrative centre, and symbol of personal prestige. Often built atop hills, cliffs, or fortified terrain, with features such as a keep, garrison, armory, walls, towers, and gatehouses. Emphasizes individual authority and military strength over communal heritage.
