Reference

#### Spirit
_Incorporeal entity_
Spirits embody the essence of life, death, or nature. They range from benevolent guides to hostile remnants, and can take many forms.
- Ancestral Spirit: Guardian of a family, clan, or bloodline. Offers guidance, protection, or warnings.
- Elemental Spirit: Manifestation of natural forces—wind, water, fire, or earth. May be neutral or destructive.
- Restless Spirit: Souls unsettled by trauma, injustice, or unfinished business. Often haunt specific locations, objects, or individuals.

#### Spectre
_Undead, incorporeal_
Malignant spirits bound to hatred, tragedy, or unfinished vengeance. Spectres are shadows of their former selves, often dangerous to the living.
- Vengeful Spectre: Pursues those responsible for their death or suffering. Violent and unpredictable.
- Haunting Spectre: Bound to a location, object, or event, endlessly replaying moments of demise.
- Wraith: A powerful and malevolent spectre capable of draining life, cursing the living, or influencing the material world.

#### Draugr
_Undead, corporeal_
Reanimated warriors or notable figures twisted by undeath. Draugr retain physical form and combat skill, but are relentless guardians of tombs, cursed treasures, or ancient secrets.
- Tomb Draugr: Protect burial sites or relics. Slow-moving but heavily armed and armored.
- Frenzied Draugr: Driven mad by undeath, attacking anything in sight.
- Champion Draugr: Elite warriors or leaders. Retain exceptional skill, tactics, and combat prowess from life.

#### Forsworn
##### Oathguard
_Fallen warrior, bound by vow_
These warriors died but refused [[Valhalla]] because of an unbroken promise. They remain tied to battlefields, graves, or family halls, guarding what they swore to protect.
- Combat: Skilled and disciplined, wielding ghostly versions of their old arms.
- Behaviour: Honourable and proud, willing to test or speak with mortals who show respect.
- Role: Guardians of sacred sites, ancient treasures, or bloodlines.
##### Godsworn
_Fallen warrior, defiant_
The Godsworn rejected not just [[Valhalla]], but the gods themselves. They walk the world in defiance, seeking their own fate apart from [[Odin]]’s call.
- Combat: Fierce, bitter, and relentless, their spectral weapons glow with wrath.
- Behaviour: Distrustful of divine power, harsh in judgment, but sometimes willing to strike deals.
- Role: Leaders of wandering warbands, cursed champions, or enemies of the gods.
##### Barrow-Host
_Fallen warrior, unbound_
The Barrow-Host are warriors who turned away from [[Valhalla]] but lost their sense of self. They haunt tombs and old battlefields, repeating the fights of their final day.
- Combat: Dangerous in groups, fighting with half-remembered skill and ghostly blades.
- Behaviour: Confused and broken, sometimes mistaking the living for old foes or comrades.
- Role: Haunting spirits, tragic remnants of forgotten wars, or swarming threats to explorers.
