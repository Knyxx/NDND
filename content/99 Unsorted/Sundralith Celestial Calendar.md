#### Sól 🌞 & Máni 🌙 — The Celestial Calendar
- **Celestial Roles:**
	- Sól → the Sun, eternal flame, giver of life, unchanging.
	- Máni → the Moon, cyclical and mystical, guiding tides, fate, and mortal life.
- **Máni Cycle:**
	- 13 cycles per year, each month marked by one Full Moon.
	- Máni resets each month; New Moon → Dark Moon is 28 days.

#### Seasons
- Seasonal Flow:
    - Spring: Jarnmånad → Blótmånad
    - Summer: Eldmånad → Sólmånad
    - Autumn: Jægmånad → Skuggmånad
    - Winter: Høstmånad → Slutmånad
- Festival Alignment: Each Full Moon festival aligns with Nordic customs — hunting, feasting, ancestors, magic, survival.
- Rituals & Beliefs:
     Sacrifices, offerings, or feasts are tied to moonlight.
    - Omens appear in weather, animals, dreams, or natural phenomena.
    - Year begins and ends in darkness, echoing cycles of life, death, and renewal.

#### Phases of the Moon
- New 1st > 1st Q 7th > Full 14th > Last Q 21st > Dark 28th

#### Months
28 days each month
1. Jarnmånad (YARN-moh-nahd) – Blót Moon (BLOAT Moon): Renewal & offerings
2. Vetrblót (VET-er-bloat) – Frost Moon (FRAWST Moon): Endurance & hearth rituals
3. Grœnmånad (GROEN-moh-nahd) – Wolf Moon (WULF Moon): Hunt & courage
4. Blótmånad (BLOAT-moh-nahd) – Blood Moon (BLUD Moon): Conflict & oaths
5. Eldmånad (ELD-moh-nahd) – Fire Moon (FYE-er Moon): Feasts & courage
6. Stormmånad (STORM-moh-nahd) – Storm Moon (STORM Moon): Tempests & maritime omens
7. Sólmånad (SOHL-moh-nahd) – High Sun Moon (HIGH SOON Moon): Abundance & contests
8. Jægmånad (YAEG-moh-nahd) – Hunter’s Moon (HUN-terz Moon): Survival & offerings
9. Skuggmånad (SKOOG-moh-nahd) – Shadow Moon (SHAD-oh Moon): Magic & divination
10. Høstmånad (HURST-moh-nahd) – Harvest Moon (HAR-vest Moon): Reaping & gratitude
11. Minnemånad (MIN-neh-moh-nahd) – Ancestor Moon (AN-ses-ter Moon): Remembrance & lanterns
12. Langnatt (LANG-naht) – Long Night Moon (LONG NITE Moon): Winter endurance & stories
13. Slutmånad (SLUT-moh-nahd) – Veil Moon (VAYL Moon): Death, spirits & year’s end
