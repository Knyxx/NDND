#Ritual #Twin-Blessed 

Divine Ritual of [[Solaris and Lunaris]]

**~={blue}Purpose:=~**
- Infuse a [[Twin-Blessed]] item with the dual blessings of Solaris (boldness/offense) and Lunaris (protection/support).

**~={blue}Materials:=~**
- [[Twin-Blessed Celestite]]
- [[Twin-Blessed]] Item
- Sunlight or Moonlight

**~={blue}Steps:=~**
- Place the item on a pedestal and position the Celestite above it.
- Align sunlight or moonlight through the Celestite onto the item.
- Touch the item and pray to Solaris or Lunaris.
- Focus for 1 hour as the item absorbs celestial energy.

**~={blue}Effects:=~**

| Item Type                   | Solaris (Sunlight)                    | Lunaris (Moonlight)                   | Combined Sun & Full Moon                                      |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Weapon**                  | Weapon attacks gain +1d6 Fire damage  | Weapon attacks gain +1d6 Cold damage  | Weapon attacks gain +2d6 Radiant damage                       |
| **Armor / Shield**          | Fire resistance                       | Cold resistance                       | Radiant and Necrotic Resistance                               |
| **Trinket / Wondrous Item** | Enhances active abilities             | Enhances support/protective abilities | Grants minor radiant benefit and protective effect to wielder |
| **Spellcasting Focus**      | Damaging spells gain +1d6 Fire damage | Damaging spells gain +1d6 Cold damage | Damaging spells gain +2d6 radiant damage                      |

**~={blue}Duration:=~**
- One week
