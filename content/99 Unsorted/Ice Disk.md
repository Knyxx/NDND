Magic Item | Wondrous Item | Attuned

A flat iron disk with engraved frost-like patterns around a recessed central circle. Sapphire-blue runes ᛁ (Isa) and ᛒ (Berkano) shimmer coldly along the grooves.

Attuned: Grants resistance to cold damage; weapon attacks deal +1d4 cold damage.
