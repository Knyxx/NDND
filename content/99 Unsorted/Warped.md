Reference

Flora and fauna twisted by [[Chaos Energy]], exhibiting unpredictable mutations and magical effects.

Examples:
- Plants: [[Bloodvine]], [[Glassbark Trees]], [[Chaos Blossoms]]
- Animals: [[Shardback Lizard]], [[Flicker Hound]], [[Warped Stag]]

Effects:
- Small mutations: subtle changes, erratic behaviour
- Medium mutations: visible mutations, minor environmental warping
- Large mutations: dangerous creatures, terrain hazards, unstable magic

[[Warped]] radiate instability and may appear anywhere near [[Dimensional Breach|fractures or rifts]].
