Settlement | Village

Small settlement dedicated to [[Selûne]], run by the [[Luminous Wardens]].

Members of [[Ember Order]] partnered with the wardens to convert [[Chaos Energy]] to Natural Vitality, managing to create the [[Robust Multitool]] and upgrade [[Robust.Garden.Bot (RGB)]].

Abandoned following news of an incoming attack.
