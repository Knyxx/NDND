#### Hook
Ore shipments for [[Ironpeak Forge]] (west) and finished weapons from [[Hearthfire Smiths]] (east) have been stolen by bandits. The forges’ owners need them recovered quickly.

#### Objective
Recover stolen ore and weapons, identify the bandits, and decide whether to bring them to justice or negotiate.

#### Key NPCs
- [[Borin Stonehand]] – Ironpeak Forge (dwarf, mining tools & armor)  
- [[Thalia Stonehand]] – Ironpeak Forge (dwarf, mining & smithing expert)
- [[Eirik Firevein]] – Hearthfire Smiths (human, weapons & trade goods)
- [[Svanhild Ashen]] – Hearthfire Smiths (human, weapons & household tools)
- [[Korran Blackfang]] – bandit leader, ex-miner
- [[Syla “Icehand” Vex]] – bandit lieutenant, traps & ambushes
- [[Rurik and Hela]] – bandit grunts

#### Quest Steps
1. Gather info in encampment: forge owners, caravan drivers, militia.
2. Track bandits along cliffs and forested slopes, avoiding predators and traps.
3. Scout camp: observe numbers, routines, optional stealth.
4. Confrontation or negotiation:
    - Direct assault: fight bandits and recover goods
    - Stealth/subterfuge: steal or sabotage
    - Diplomacy: offer terms for release of goods
5. Return recovered goods to forges, decide fate of bandits.
6. Optional twist: bandits act for rival dwarven clan or external trader.

#### Challenges
- Mountain ambushes or natural hazards (cliffs, icy streams)
- Traps and lookouts at hideout
- Combat with bandit NPCs or wild predators
- Negotiation or intimidation checks if diplomacy is attempted

#### Rewards
- Gold or valuable trade goods
- Favors or discounts from forges
- Reputation boost with militia and merchants
- Clues to larger political or clan intrigue
