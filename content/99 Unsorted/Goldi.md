The spirit of a young girl who died while hiding in a cave waiting for her parents, she haunts the caverns next to the [[Lost Frozen City]].

Died form the cold while waiting for her parents.

Body was eaten by bears which she cursed on first rising as a banshee, turning them into her undead servants.
