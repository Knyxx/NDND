A fractured, geode-like meteor born from unstable magics, numerous enchanted items, and a chaotic interdimensional portal that brought it—and its wielder—into this world. Its cracked surface features a large maw revealing a roiling void at its core, while partially hardened, molten layers flow around and through each other, occasionally glimpsing the consuming darkness within.

#### Attunement: 
- While attuned, the amulet's layers animate, generating powers and dreams.

#### Properties:
- Arcane Attunement: Reattune the amulet during a long rest to change its element (fire, cold, or lightning).
- Arcane Empowerment: Spells deal +1d4 damage of the current element once per turn.
- Arcane Shield: Start of turn, gain resistance to the next damage of the current element.
- Arcane Echo: Proficiency in Arcana.
- Foresight Drain: On a failed save, see visions of a lost future; multiple failures per day cause 1 level of exhaustion.
