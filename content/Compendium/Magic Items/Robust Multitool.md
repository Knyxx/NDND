A multifunctional druidic focus and holy symbol of [[Selûne]], integrating gardening tools with magical crystal nodes. Silver inlays depict crescent moons and star motifs along the prongs and frame, symbolizing [[Selûne]]’s guidance and protection. The focus channels [[Chaos Energy]] into natural vitality, nurturing plants, soil, and living beings.

Integrated into [[Robust.Garden.Bot (RGB)]].
