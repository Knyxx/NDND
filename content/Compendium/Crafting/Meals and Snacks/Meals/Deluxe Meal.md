Crafting and Usage modified by your Proficiency with Cook's Utensils.

- Gain 2d12 + (Proficiency Bonus x 3) temporary hit points.
- Gain Advantage on your next Saving Throw.
- Gain +2 bonus to AC for 6 hours.
- Spoils in 6 hours if not consumed.
