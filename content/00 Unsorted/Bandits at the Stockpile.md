Encounter | 6×L3 Easy

#### Setting
The party comes upon a half-collapsed ruin where bandits are hurriedly clearing a hidden stockpile. Crates are stacked, racks dismantled, and a cart is being loaded. Broken walls, fallen pillars, and debris provide abundant cover. The bandits are focused on the job, not on security.

Scimitar + Shortsword
- Both light, can attack with both in a turn, action and bonus action
- Scimitar (Nick) attack hits: Shortsword (Light) attack can be part of the action, freeing up bonus action
- Shortsword (Vex) attack hits: advantage on next attack roll against this target, until the end of next turn


Action: Attack w Scimitar (Light weapon) allows extra attack as a bonus action
Bonus Action


Scimitar - Finesse, Light, Nick
Shortsword - Finesse, Light, Vex


Nick - Scimitar
When you make an attack with a weapon that has the Light property, you can use a Bonus Action to make one attack with a different Light weapon you’re wielding.
The Nick mastery property allows you to make the additional attack you receive from wielding two Light weapons as part of the initial attack action.
This doesn’t mean you can make a third attack as a Bonus Action, as the Light property specifies you only get one extra attack. But, while it may not pump your damage, this frees up your Bonus Action to use class/species abilities, such as the Rogue’s Cunning Action, while still getting an additional attack in.

Vex - Shortsword
When you hit a creature and deal damage, this mastery property gives you Advantage on your next attack roll before the end of your next turn. This is an excellent way for Rogues to all but guarantee a Sneak Attack hit on their next turn.










#### Tactics
- Two Scouts are on lookout — one in a crude watchtower, one patrolling the camp perimeter
- Thugs lounge near the fire and rush to engage once an alarm is raised
- Gorrin, the captain, joins when the first thug falls or if the party gets close to his tent
- Scouts focus fire on lightly armored or spellcasting PCs
- Gorrin uses his Multiattack aggressively but will retreat if reduced below half HP, shouting for his men to “cover the boss.”
- If the party sneaks in quietly, they can ambush and eliminate a scout before combat begins.

#### XP Calculation:
Total Monster XP = 700 + (3 × 100) + (2 × 100) = 1,300
Number of monsters = 6 → Multiplier = 1.5 (per DMG p.82)
Adjusted XP: 1,300 × 1.5 = 1,950 XP → Easy for 6 level 3 PCs

#### Hidden Cache (optional side reward)

If the party investigates the clue and searches the area (DC 12 Investigation or Survival), they find a disturbed patch of earth behind the largest standing stone.

Contents:
- A small iron coffer (locked, DC 13 Thieves’ Tools or can be forced open with DC 15 Strength).
- Inside:
    - 85 gp and 240 sp in a canvas pouch
    - A **Ring of Warmth** (tarnished but functional)
    - A **sealed letter** bearing the merchant guild’s crest, mentioning a caravan route — possibly a lead to another Redjaw hideout or future ambush site

**Optional complication:**  
As the PCs uncover the chest, a **hidden bandit lookout** (Scout) or **forest wolf** (use _Wolf_ stat block) may approach, drawn by the noise or scent, creating a brief follow-up encounter.
