Reference

Massive, powerful creatures that enter the world through [[Dimensional Breach|large rifts]], leaving destruction and chaos in their wake.

Nature: Colossal apex predators or elemental entities, often with magical or chaotic traits.

Origin: Travel from other planes via [[Dimensional Breach]], usually linked to planar disturbances or magical disasters.

Behaviour: Mostly solitary; their presence warps ecosystems, mutates wildlife, and destabilizes local magic.

Traits: Enormous strength, thick hides, natural armour, and sometimes innate magical abilities; leave residual rift energies in their path.

Hazards: Deadly to settlements and adventurers alike; encounters require caution, preparation, and strategy.

Legends: Seen as omens, divine instruments, or harbingers of chaos; some cultures worship them, others hunt them.
