[[Robust.Garden.Bot (RGB)]] started out as helper for [[enclave]].

[[Luminous Wardens]] and [[Ember Order]] members who also followed [[Selûne]], experimented on [[Robust.Garden.Bot (RGB)|RGB]], managing to create a tool capable of converting [[Chaos Energy]] into natural vitality, [[Robust Multitool]].

[[enclave]] received word of an attack and abandoned it, though the attack never came, [[Robust.Garden.Bot (RGB)|RGB]] eventually fell into a stasis.

Many years later, [[Ember Order]] members dedicated to [[Shar]] found [[Robust.Garden.Bot (RGB)|RGB]], and began trying to reverse engineer the technology, wiping most of [[Robust.Garden.Bot (RGB)|RGB]]'s memory in the process, eventually returning [[Robust.Garden.Bot (RGB)|RGB]] to stasis in a remote village.

The village was later destroyed by [[Ember Order]] members on [[Selûne]]'s orders, who wished for [[Robust.Garden.Bot (RGB)|RGB]] to be left in peace, along with a [[Weathered Chapel Token]] from the [[enclave]] they once served.
