Cursed Item | Magic Item | Wondrous Item, Uncommon | Attuned

#### Description
A darkened silver hairpin shaped like a bird with wings arched upward, their tips meeting to form a circular gem setting. Though unfinished, it sharpens the wearer’s eyes for glimmers in the dark.

#### Properties
- You have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks to notice valuables, hidden compartments, or small reflective objects.
- In dim light or darkness, your eyes faintly glint, granting a +2 bonus to passive Perception.

#### Gem Setting
- Attunement: During a long rest, slot, remove, or change gems in the gem socket.
- Empty: The hollow bezel tugs at your mind. You have disadvantage on saving throws against being charmed.
- Filled: Gain benefits based on slotted gem, though only one can be slotted at a time.

#### Cursed
- This item is cursed and cannot be removed.
