Magic Item | Wondrous Item | Attuned

A thin, flowing cloak that seems woven from mist and shadow. When worn, the wearer’s movements leave faint ghostly trails that shimmer in dim light. Once per long rest, the wearer can cast Misty Step. The cloak is intangible to the touch, slightly cold, and seems to ripple with every breeze.

Flavor: Worn by enforcers of spectral warriors, it carries a whisper of their swiftness and elusiveness.
