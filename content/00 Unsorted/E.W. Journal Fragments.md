Journal fragments found among notes of [[Tor's Journal]].

#### Fragment 1 – Observation

_Old, crinkled, torn fragment. Small doodles of helmets and ghostly arms margin the page. A name freshly written and circled at the top—[[Edrin the Wayfarer]]._

I have travelled old battlefields and ruined villages, and I have seen the dead walk. At first, I thought them all the same—shadows of souls lingering too long. But patterns are emerging.

Some linger near mounds or hamlets, silent and watchful. I have seen them nudge children back to safety and drive off beasts, though they strike the disrespectful without hesitation.

Others blaze with fire or frost, pacing constantly, muttering curses backward, eyes burning like coals. They seem to hate priests and holy folk most of all, yet they strike only within certain areas, as if the land itself keeps them tethered.

And then there are the ones that wail and scream, charging at shadows, striking at nothing—or everything—lost in some endless replay of battles fought long ago. They cannot leave the field where they fell, and some travellers escape only because of that.

I can sketch them roughly, but the drawings capture little. I do not yet know what to call them, only that they are different—and that care must be taken when passing through their domains.


#### Fragment 2 – Classification

_Margins filled with stars, arrows, and rough sketches of ghostly figures. Notes scribbled over each other in excitement._

After weeks of careful watching, I feel I can begin to classify them:

###### The Watchers
- Stand rigid, eyes steady, footfalls soft.  
- Sometimes hum faint tunes, like lullabies or marching songs.
- Protect villages, graves, and children; punish thieves or the disrespectful.
- Watchful, unaccepting of disrespect or falsehood within their domain.

###### The Defiers
- Wreathed in smoke or frost.
- Fingers twitch near weapons, low bitter laughter, mutters prayers backward.
- Hate holy folk and pursue intruders only within their domains.
- Defiant, quick to lash out, unforgiving of holiness within their domain, especially the [[Old Norse Pantheon]].

###### The Lost
- Faces blurred, shifting. Phantom weapons strike at shadows.
- Wail, whisper, cry, fight endlessly.
- Bound to the battlefield or tomb where they died; cannot leave, even in pursuit.
- Poor lost souls, bound to a place, filled only with blind rage.

Three names, three faces of refusal. I shall use these to remember who is who—and perhaps survive encounters in the future.


#### Fragment 3 – Survival

_Page torn from bottom, scribbled in haste, ink smudged_

Curiosity can be dangerous, yet some knowledge may save a life and I am not the only curious fool it seems. I met a priest from the [[Brotherhood]], and together our observations paint a clearer picture. Their order has long recognized these spirits and assigned names to their ways, ways I must learn of.

###### Oathsworn
- Refused the call of [[Valhalla]] to keep an oath.
- Truth-seekers and defenders; unlikely to attack unless provoked.
- Speak truth, bow if possible, keep weapons visible. Respect rewarded; disrespect punished.
- Some have been welcomed into village rites; I even witnessed one take part in a wedding, perhaps honouring an ancestor.

###### Godsworn
- Refused [[Valhalla]] and the [[Gods]] in defiance.
- Avoid flaunting holy symbols; words are dangerous and false promises remembered.
- May bargain if you share their disdain for the gods—but the price is high.
- Example: a priest mocked a shrine, the Godsworn struck; a merchant bowed and fled, spared.

###### Barrow-host
- Too bound to their final fight to enter [[Valhalla]], they continue battling eternally, blind to their target.
- Retreat is often wiser than attack; groups improve chances.
- Noise, bells, or reflective surfaces may distract.
- Bound to the site of death; some escape only because the spirit cannot leave its domain.
