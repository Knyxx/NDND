Settlement | Encampment

#### Type
- Mountain encampment and fortified trade hub

#### Location
- Broad hollow in the [[Varkundr]] mountains where three passes meet.
- Western cliff conceals a sealed old mine.
- Cold, high-altitude climate; long winters, short summers, snowstorms, and strong winds.

#### Population & Society
- Mainly [[Human]], with [[Dwarf|Dwarves]], [[Orc|Orcs]], and other hardy mountain folk.
- Factions: militia, merchant guilds, small clans.
- Traditions: seasonal feasts, Sky Festival, ancestor rituals, warrior contests.

#### Landmarks & Key Locations
- [[Crossroads Square]]: central hub of commerce and social life, stalls and temporary market tents.
- Central Inn – [[The Frosted Horn]]: large timber hall with gabled roof; innkeepers [[Hilda Stormbrand]] and [[Tom Stormbrand]].
- Old Mine: sealed iron-banded entrance on western cliff, guarded by local militia.
- Western Smithy – [[Ironpeak Forge]]: located next to the old mine, run by [[Dwarf|dwarves]] [[Borin Stonehand]] and [[Thalia Stonehand]]; specializes in mining tools, armor, and ironwork.
- Eastern Smithy – [[Hearthfire Smiths]]: situated near the eastern gate, run by [[Human|humans]] [[Eirik Firevein]] and [[Svanhild Ashen]], focused on weapons, household tools, and trade goods.
- Watchtowers: strategically placed around the encampment with beacon fires for vigilance.
- Decorative & Ceremonial Sites: rune-carved stones, horned beams, ceremonial totems scattered across the hollow.
- Natural Features: pine forests, waterfalls, snow-capped cliffs surrounding the settlement.

#### Economy & Resources
- Mining, timber, animal husbandry, smithing, woodworking.
- Trade hub for ores, timber, livestock, and artisan goods.

#### Hazards
- Predators, bandits, rival clans.
- Occasional supernatural phenomena or magical anomalies.
- Political tensions among factions or travelers.

#### History & Lore
- Founded as a mining outpost; fortified after [[Elderrend]] (998 AD).
- Legends of mountain spirits and the sealed mine.

#### Story Hooks
- Explore the sealed mine for treasure.
- Resolve faction disputes or escort caravans.
- Investigate frost wards or mountain spirits.
- Trade disputes or wilderness threats.
