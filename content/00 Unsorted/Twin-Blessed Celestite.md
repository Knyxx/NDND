Magic Item | Ritual Component, Very Rare | Attuned

#### Description
Ancestral Heirloom of the [[Mistwalker Clan]].
A perfectly cut Celestite crystal, seeming to always capture light, as if sunlight and moonlight were drawn within its facets. When aligned with the sun or moon, it splits their light into twin beams, channelling divine energy into twin-blessed items.
