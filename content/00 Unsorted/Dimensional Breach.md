Reference

Fractures are the smallest manifestations of dimensional instability—hairline cracks in reality. They first appeared following the [[Sundrbjǫrg Breach]], when a being from another dimension forced passage through an unstable portal, rupturing the veil and scattering chaos.

[[Chaos Energy]] leaked through these fractures, [[Warped|mutating creatures]] and pooling in barren regions. Over time, fractures widened into rifts—stable tears large enough for extraplanar creatures to pass.

Rifts vary in size, from openings admitting minor pests to apocalyptic breaches through which world-altering beings emerge. Whether these tears are natural or deliberately caused remains debated.

##### Classification of Dimensional Breaches

###### Fracture
- Description: Microscopic tear; leaks only chaotic energy
- Maximum Passage: None
###### Small Rift
- Description: Minor opening; unstable, flickering
- Maximum Passage: Small creatures
	- Imps – mischievous fiendish scouts
	- Quasits – tiny chaotic familiars
	- Mephits – elemental tricksters (dust, smoke, ice, etc.)
	- Warped vermin – rats, birds, or insects mutated by chaos energy
###### Medium Rift
- Description: Stable gap in the veil
- Maximum Passage: Medium creatures
	- Githyanki warriors – planar raiders
	- Shadow demons – incorporeal fiends
	- Star Spawn Grue / Mangler – minor Far Realm horrors
	- Fiend-touched humanoids – cultists or planar soldiers
###### Large Rift
- Description: Widened breach; difficult to contain
- Maximum Passage: Large creatures
	- Oni – ogre-mage outsiders
	- Barbed devils – devil soldiers
	- Slaads (Blue/Red) – chaotic frog-like creatures
	- Nightmares – infernal mounts
###### Huge Rift
- Description: Towering tear, radiating energy
- Maximum Passage: Huge creatures
	- Balors – apex demons
	- Pit fiends – devil commanders
	- Fomorians – twisted giants
	- Death Slaads / Titans – large chaotic entities
###### Gargantuan Rift
- Description: Catastrophic rupture altering whole regions
- Maximum Passage: Gargantuan beings
	- Astral Dreadnoughts – planar predators
	- Kraken – massive sea monsters
	- Ancient Dragons – planar or chaos-infused
	- Tarrasque – planet-scale destructive force
