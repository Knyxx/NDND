Settlement | Fortress

[[Stonehowe]], southern [[Karnvald]]

#### Overview:
[[Ashhold]] is the ancestral seat of the [[Ashwardens]], established as a fortified foothold following the first emergence of the [[Restless]] from the [[Gravedeep]] after [[Svartdomr]]. Known as the Gate of the Veil, the fortress guards the boundary between the living and the dead, ensuring that restless spirits remain bound to their proper resting places beneath [[Stonehowe]].

#### Founding and Architecture:
Founded [[_timeline|2 CY]] in response to the sudden threat of the restless dead, [[Ashhold]]’s walls are carved from reinforced stone and woven into the surrounding cairns, blending natural formations with deliberate construction. Towers rise above the fissures and chasms leading into the catacombs below, enabling constant vigilance. Inside, barracks, armouries, training halls, and ritual chambers reflect both martial and spiritual functions. Runic carvings, ash-laden ceremonial halls, and sacred symbols emphasize the fortress’s role as a guardian of the Veil.

#### Historical Significance:
- [[Svartdomr]]: [[Ashhold]] was established as a bastion against the emerging [[Restless]] in the chaos following the war.
- Siege of the [[Restless]]: A major assault by a horde of aggressive spirits tested the fortress’s defenses, cementing its reputation as nearly impenetrable.
- Founding of the [[Ashwardens]] [[_timeline|4 CY]]: The fortress became the permanent seat of the order, codifying rituals, training, and governance under the first [[Ash Queen]].

#### Cultural Significance:
Communities such as [[Moundstead]] revere [[Ashhold]] as a living sentinel. Villagers offer food, coin, and symbolic gifts to honor the Ashwardens’ vigilance. Folklore portrays the fortress as imbued with the will of generations who have guarded the threshold between life and death.

#### Role and Function:
[[Ashhold]] continues to serve as the operational center of the [[Ashwardens]]. Its inhabitants dedicate themselves to:
- Settling the [[Restless]] within the [[Gravedeep]]
- Training recruits in combat, ritual, and spirit-binding
- Conducting sacred rites within the ritual chambers
- Maintaining surveillance and defense of the catacomb fissures

The fortress embodies both martial might and spiritual authority, standing as a symbol of the enduring vigilance required to preserve the Veil and safeguard the living from the encroaching dead.
