Skywatch Fortress (_SKY-watch_) - "Fortress of the Clouds"

#### Overview  
Founded in [[_timeline|203 AD]] by the [[Cloudrunner Clan]], Skywatch Fortress stood as both a mountain stronghold and a twin temple devoted to [[Solaris and Lunaris]]. Carved into the heights of [[Mount Dawnveil]] in the [[Jade Peaks]], it overlooked the valley below and safeguarded [[Cloudrest Village]]. The fortress endured for generations as the clan’s seat of strength and faith until its fall, when a devastating assault brought the Cloudrunners to ruin.  

#### Significance  
- Strategic Stronghold: Controlled mountain passes and trade routes.
- Twin Temple: Featured sanctuaries aligned with sun and moonlight; rituals centered on celestial events.
- Cultural Seat: Preserved Cloudrunner lore, songs, and genealogies.
- Mythic Role: Legends speak of the Twins appearing in rare alignments above its towers, their laughter echoing in the winds.
- Legacy: Its fall ended Cloudrunner dominion; the ruins endure as a haunted reminder of their rise and collapse.
