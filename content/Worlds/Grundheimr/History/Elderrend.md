## Description

Elderrend (_EL-der-rend_) — “The Rending Flame”

Date: Thor’s Day, Day 11, Victorymoon, 998 AD
Duration: 1½ days (+ 1 week of aftershocks)

## Overview

Elderrend was a series of cataclysmic magical explosions that ravaged [[Sundralith]], unleashing corrosive energies that scorched forests, fractured stone, and turned rivers into acidic torrents. The main event lasted a day and a half, with aftershocks continuing for an additional week.

## Effects

- Environment: Forests were reduced to stumps, hills cracked, and waterways poisoned. Recovery is slow in many areas, with traces of corrosive magic persisting.
- Arcane: Ley lines and ruins were saturated with residual energy, producing unpredictable magical phenomena.
- Flora & Fauna: Many species were eradicated or displaced. Rift zones now host mutated creatures and warped plant growth.
- Society: Survivors retreated to fortified settlements; trade and travel were heavily disrupted, and fear of anomalies spread widely.

## Timeline

- Day 1 — First Blasts: Forests and hills devastated; rivers turned acidic; ley lines flare.
- Day 2 — Peak Rending: Widespread rifts tear the land apart; flora and fauna collapse; survivors scramble to safety.
- Aftershocks (1 week): Residual magical energy causes sporadic explosions and anomalies; mutated creatures and warped plants appear; travel and trade remain disrupted.

## Aftermath

Elderrend shares notable parallels with Svartdomr, hinting at recurring cycles of arcane catastrophe in Sundralith.
Its scars endure: unstable rifts and lingering magic continue to shape the land, its inhabitants, and the study of arcane forces.
