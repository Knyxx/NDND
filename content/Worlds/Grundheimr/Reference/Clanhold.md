## Description

A fortified ancestral home of a clan or tribe, held collectively to preserve heritage, culture, and survival. Serves as a defensive refuge, communal hub, and spiritual centre, often adapted to mountains, forests, or caverns. Typical features include a great hall, longhouses, workshops, shrines, and hidden passages, emphasizing function and tradition over individual prestige.
