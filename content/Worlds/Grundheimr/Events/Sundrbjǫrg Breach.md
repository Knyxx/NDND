Event | Catastrophe | [[_timeline#3 BC|3 BC]]

#### Overview
The earliest recorded arcane disruption in [[Sundralith]], triggered by unstable energies within a trans dimensional portal and the arrival of [[Wuskan Dhyrktelonis]]. This event tore open the first [[Dimensional Breach]], is linked to the eruption of [[Sundrbjǫrg]], and a catastrophic impact in the [[Varkundr]] mountains.

#### Effects
- Environment: Violent eruption of [[Sundrbjǫrg]], avalanches, fractured terrain in the [[Varkundr]] range.
Arcane: First documented [[Dimensional Breach]]; scattered records note pervasive [[Chaos Energy]].
- Flora & Fauna: Local mutations; disturbed or awakened spirits.
- Society: Early settlements displaced; later regarded as a precursor to wider calamities.

#### Context & Legacy
Predating [[Svartdomr]], this event represents the first known incursion of chaos. Surviving records and oral traditions note the eruption and aftermath, with the resulting fracture leaving lasting instability in the [[Varkundr]] mountains and dormant energies that persist to this day.
