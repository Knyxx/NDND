Elderrend (_EL-der-rend_) - “The Rending Flame”

#### Overview
Elderrend was a series of cataclysmic magical explosions that devastated [[Sundralith]], releasing corrosive energy that scorched forests, fractured stone, and turned rivers into acidic torrents. The blasts tore rifts across the land, leaving enduring environmental and arcane instability.

#### Effects
- Environment: Forests reduced to stumps, hills cracked, waterways poisoned; slow recovery in some areas, though traces of corrosive magic remain.
- Arcane: Ley lines and ruins are saturated with residual energy, producing unpredictable magical phenomena.
- Flora & Fauna: Many species were eradicated or displaced; mutated creatures and warped plant growth now inhabit rift zones.
- Society: Survivors established fortified settlements; trade and travel were disrupted, and fear of anomalies spread widely.

#### Aftermath
- Elderrend shares notable similarities with Svartdomr, suggesting recurring cycles of arcane disasters in Sundralith.
- The scars of Elderrend endure: unstable rifts and lingering magic continue to shape the land, its inhabitants, and the study of arcane forces.
