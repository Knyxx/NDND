Svartdomr (_SVART-dohm_) — “Black Doom”

#### Overview
A decade of devastation across [[Sundralith]], marked by escalating [[Dimensional Breach]], surges of [[Chaos Energy]], and the appearance of [[Great Beasts]] and [[Restless]]. The calamity ended with the rise of the [[Five Kings]], though its resolution remains unclear.

#### Effects
- Environment: Twisted terrain, unnatural flora, alien skies.
- Arcane: Fractured ley lines, unstable magic, awakened spirits.
- Flora & Fauna: Mutated creatures, new beasts, corrupted spirits.
- Society: Collapse of kingdoms, rise of survivor orders (e.g. Golden Ascendency), emergence of empowered individuals.

#### Historical Context
The first widespread eruption of chaos in recorded history. Precedes the creation of the [[Sundralith Celestial Calendar]] and is often compared to later upheavals such as [[Elderrend]].

#### Legacy
Lingering mutated creatures, unstable ley lines, and restless spirits. The true source of the chaos remains unknown.
