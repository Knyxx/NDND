Religion | Faerûnian Pantheon

Part of the [[Faerûnian Pantheon]].

Selûne is the goddess of the moon, stars, navigation, and prophecy.

Revered as a benevolent deity of light, she represents guidance, protection for travellers, and the cyclical nature of life.

Often depicted as a radiant woman with silver hair and a crescent moon, her followers include sailors, wanderers, and those seeking hope in darkness.

Her eternal rival is [Shar], goddess of darkness and loss.
