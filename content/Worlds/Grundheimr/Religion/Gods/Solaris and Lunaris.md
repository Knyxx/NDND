Religion | Grundheimr Pantheon
![[Solaris and Lunaris.png]]

Solaris and Lunaris are worshiped as a dual god, most often spoken of as The Twins. Their presence represents both the forging of paths and the tending of what is left in their wake. Solaris is remembered in myth as the one who carves the way forward, shaping the world with fire and brilliance. Lunaris is honored as the one who follows with patience, gathering and guiding all that lingers behind.

Though mortals speak of them as sun and moon, in truth they are distant stars, paired across the firmament. Their influence is traced through rare blooms that open in twilight, shadows that bend unnaturally, or comets that seem to linger and loop — subtle signs that those with sharp eyes interpret as the quiet interplay of the two.

Legends often describe their laughter, for the Twins are amused by mortal certainty. People see only what they wish — balance, order, prophecy, gender, and role — and mistake their blindness for wisdom. The Twins play with this folly, leaving hidden patterns in light and shadow that are rarely recognized for what they are.

Among the esoteric traditions, whispered only in hidden circles, there persists a more daring truth: that the Twins are not brother and sister, nor mirror halves, but women bound as eternal lovers, their secret carried unseen in the stars.
