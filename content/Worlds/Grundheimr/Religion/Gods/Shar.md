Religion | Faerûnian Pantheon

[[Shar]] is the goddess of darkness, loss, night, and secrets.

She embodies shadow, despair, and the hidden aspects of existence, often opposing her sister [[Selûne]], goddess of the moon.

Depicted as a dark-robed figure with an obscured or shadowed face, [[Shar]] is worshiped by those who embrace secrecy, ambition, or the inevitability of endings.

Her influence spreads in loss, grief, and the concealment of truth.
