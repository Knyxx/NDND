The Faerûnian Pantheon was the dominant pantheon worshiped across Faerûn, a continent on Toril.

- [[Selûne]] – Goddess of the moon, Selûne was revered in three main aspects: as a mother goddess overseeing reproductive cycles; as a guide for navigators and sailors; and as a protector for those seeking guidance or safety in darkness.    
- Shar – Neutral evil goddess of darkness, loss, and the subterranean realms of Faerûn. She was worshiped by those who laboured in darkness, such as miners, and by those seeking to relinquish memories, recover lost objects, or endure deep despair.
