A humble but well-made chef’s knife with a handle of polished ox horn, bearing a simple engraving of the [[Oxfell Clan]] crest. It once belonged to [[Thyra Oxfell]], used daily to prepare meals for her son.

**Quiet Magic**
When you prepare and eat a meal you have just cooked using this knife, you gain Simple Satisfaction: minor, non-magical discomfort—aches, chill, travel weariness, or hunger—eases. This has no effect on conditions or Exhaustion.

While attuned, once per Long Rest, after eating such a meal, you gain advantage on your next saving throw made before your next rest.

Can be used as cook’s utensils for any task that requires them.
