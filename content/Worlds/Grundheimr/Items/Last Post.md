Once carried by [[Brukk Oxfell]], who bore it in defence of his family — a duty he fulfilled, though it cost him his life.

A square iron head is fixed to a thick, weathered oak haft, with an iron band near the grip stamped with the mark of the [[Oxfell Clan]]. Forged to drive posts deep into stubborn earth, it was also meant to stand where the clan stands — between home and the things that hunt it.

If you move at least 5 feet toward a creature before making an attack on your turn, the attack deals additional damage equal to twice your proficiency bonus.

While attuned, you gain a +1 bonus to Strength saving throws.

When an effect would move you against your will, you may reduce the distance moved by 10 feet, to a minimum of 0 feet.
