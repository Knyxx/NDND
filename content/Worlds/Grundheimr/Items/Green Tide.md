## Description

A torn strip of dark green cloth bound with strained silver thread. Faint runes pulse slowly along the stitching like a weak heartbeat. When empty, it hangs limp and cold. When holding items, it tightens slightly, as if afraid of being opened.

## Properties

**Hollow Space**
While worn, the sash can hold up to 150 lbs and 20 cubic feet of material.
Items may only be placed in or taken from the sash while it is worn.

**Clinging Curse**
If the sash contains items, it cannot be removed without a successful DC 20 Strength check.
On a failed attempt, the creature takes 2d6 necrotic damage, and the sash resists removal.

If the sash is removed while containing items, it attempts to devour its contents.
For non-magical items, make a DC 10 Charisma saving throw for each item. Failed saves destroy the item; successful saves cause the item to be expelled intact.

Placing the sash inside another extra dimensional container or similar effect destroys both items, anything contained, and tears a minor dimensional rift.
