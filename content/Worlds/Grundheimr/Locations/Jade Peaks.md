## Description

The Jade Peaks are a jagged mountain range shrouded in mist, with spiky cliffs rising above rolling hills that descend into river-dotted deltas. The terrain is treacherous, the air thick with fog, and the landscape is dotted with isolated villages, hidden paths, and ruins of forgotten civilizations. The peaks are both beautiful and dangerous, steeped in legend and local superstition.

The range has long served as a natural barrier and refuge. Ancient jade temples and ruins hint at a vanished civilization, while mist-hidden passes have guided traders, exiles, and adventurers for centuries. Locals maintain lore of spirits, hidden trails, and rare herbs, passing knowledge that allows survival in the perilous mountains and misted valleys.
