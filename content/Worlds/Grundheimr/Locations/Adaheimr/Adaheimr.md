Adaheimr _(AH-dah-hame)_ — “Home/realm of Ada”

#### Overview
Named for [[Paladin King Ada]] who settled the region, bring the warring clans to peace with the [[Adaheimr Treaty]] following [[Svartdomr]].
Fjorded and mountainous northern region of [[Sundralith]].

#### Geography & Environment
- Terrain: Fjords, glaciers, mountains, and taiga forests
- Climate: Harsh winters, short cool summers; blizzards, avalanches, and sea storms
- Borders: N – icy seas | E – ??? | S – [[Karnhavn]] (ruined) | W – ???
- Hazards: Frozen rivers, rough seas, avalanches, blizzards, clan feuds, monsters
- Resources: Timber, furs, fish, iron ore, frost crystals, enchanted rune-stones

#### People & Culture
- Races: primarily [[Human]], [[Dwarf]], [[Goliath]], [[Orc]]
- Society: Clans, merchants, skalds; governed by clan councils & jarls
- Language: Northern Common & Old Sundralithic
- Traditions: Rune-carving, winter festivals, ancestor feasts; ancestor & elemental worship
- Trade: Timber, furs, salted fish, carved rune-stones
- Threats: Frost wolves, ice trolls, wyverns

#### Points of Interest & History
- [[Adahowe]] – resting place of [[Paladin King Ada]]
- [[Crossroads Encampment]] - mining camp converted to small town and refugee
- [[Isvik]] – coastal fishing & shipbuilding hub (destroyed)
- [[Kaldheim]] – fortified capital city
- Historical Events: [[Svartdomr]] (CY), [[Adaheimr Treaty]] (0-6 AD), [[Elderrend]] (998 AD)
