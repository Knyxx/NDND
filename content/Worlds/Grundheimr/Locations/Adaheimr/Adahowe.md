Location | Mountain

Adahowe (_Ah-da-how_) — “Ada’s mound / barrow.”

The tomb and resting place of [[Paladin King Ada]].

Built atop [[The Oathmount]], where the [[Adaheimr Treaty]] was agreed.

#### Secret
Hidden within [[Adahowe]], built as a second seal to protect [[Dimensional Breach#Huge Rift|Great Rift]] sealed by the [[Five Kings]], and allow [[Paladin King Ada]] to guard it for eternity.

Seal locked by four keys created from equipment he used to fight the [[Great Beasts]].

Entrusted the seals to his four generals
- [[Kaelen of the Mists]] ([[Goliath]]) - _Mist-Warden_, bearer of [[Fire Disk]]
- [[Eryndor Frostmantle]] ([[Elf]]) - _Frost-Warden_, bearer of [[Ice Disk]]
- [[Grusk Oxfell]] ([[Orc]]) - _Storm-Warden_, bearer of [[Storm Disk]]
- [[Dain Emberdelve]] ([[Dwarf]]) - _Deep-Warden_, bearer of [[Acid Disk]]
