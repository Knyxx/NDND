Location | Volcano

Sundrbjǫrg (_SOON-druh-byor-g_) – “Stormed Mountain”

Volcanic peaks and rugged highlands in northern [[Sundralith]], historically associated with chaotic energies and the eruption linked to the earliest [[Dimensional Breach]].

#### Overview
Volcanic highlands where chaotic energies first breached the world, triggering mutagenic effects on local wildlife.

#### Geography & Environment
- Terrain: Volcanic mountains, rocky slopes, ash fields, sparse alpine forests
- Borders: Adjacent to the [[Varkundr]] range; northern tundra and glaciers
- Features: Eruptive craters, lava flows, avalanche-prone slopes, minor river valleys
- Hazards: Volcanic activity, sudden avalanches, unstable terrain, local rifts, residual chaotic energies

#### People & Culture
- Inhabitants: Sparse settlements of hunters, scholars, and adventurers
- Society: Mostly transient; small communities focus on resource gathering or arcane study
- Traditions: Rituals to appease restless spirits and mitigate chaotic influences
- Language: Northern Common; Old Sundralithic in scholarly texts
- Trade: Rare mineral collection, foraged herbs, runic artifacts

#### Points of Interest & History
- [[Sundrbjǫrg Peak]] – highest volcanic summit; site of the historical eruption
- Ash fields – source of unique minerals and occasional rifts
- Hidden caves – home to restless spirits and minor chaotic phenomena
- History: 3 BC eruption, earliest documented [[Dimensional Breach]], the [[Sundrbjǫrg Breach]]; precursor to [[Svartdomr]]
- Landing site for [[Wuskan Dhyrktelonis]]' entry to the world
