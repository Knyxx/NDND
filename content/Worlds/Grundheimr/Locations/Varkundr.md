Location | Mountain Range

Varkundr (_VAR-kun-der_) – “Stone Pass”

Rugged mountains at the southern edge of [[Adaheimr]], northern [[Sundralith]], defined by jagged peaks, forested valleys, and open alpine clearings.

#### Overview
Alpine and subalpine mountains forming a strategic pass and trade route between northern and southern Sundralith.

#### Geography & Environment
- Terrain: Rocky slopes, dense pine forests, natural clearings
- Borders: Frozen highlands to the north; central [[Sundralith]] lowlands to the south
- Features: Mountain passes, river valleys, abandoned mines, hidden caves, crossroads clearing for markets and caravans
- Hazards: Avalanches, rockslides, sudden storms; tales of spectral guardians and minor [[Dimensional Breach|rifts]]

#### People & Culture
- Inhabitants: Sparse settlement by miners, caravan traders, and nomadic hunters
- Society: Informal clusters of miners and traders; alliances formed around [[Crossroads Encampment]]
- Traditions: Seasonal festivals at the clearing; superstitions around spirits and mountain guardians
- Language: Northern Common; local dialects influenced by traveling merchants
- Trade: Timber, furs, minerals, and trade goods

#### Points of Interest & History
- [[Crossroads Encampment]] – central hub linking northern and southern trade routes
- Abandoned mines – remnants of early mineral extraction
- Hidden caves – rumoured to house spirits or minor rifts
- History: Mining activity led to formation of the [[Crossroads Encampment]]; long known as a strategic alpine pass
