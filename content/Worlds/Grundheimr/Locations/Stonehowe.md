Stonehowe (_STONE-how_) - "Stone Barrow"

#### Overview
Stonehowe is the ancient communal burial mound of the region. During the cataclysm of Svartdomr, fractures opened beneath the cairn, revealing the [[Gravedeep]]. The Restless dead rose from these depths until the [[Ashwardens]] reclaimed the site, establishing their fortress within the mountain.

#### Cultural Significance
Villagers of Haugar continue to honour Stonehowe with offerings and ceremonies, preserving its role as the resting place of ancestors. The cairn remains a sacred site, symbolizing the connection between the living and the dead.
