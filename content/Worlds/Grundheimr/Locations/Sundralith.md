Location | Continent

Sundralith (_SOON-drah-lith_) - "Sundered Stone"

A continent of extremes, Sundralith bears the scars of Svartdomr, its landscapes ranging from frozen fjords to temperate southern plains. Rugged mountains, deep fjords, broad river valleys, and a devastated central heartland define the terrain, fostering isolation and cultural diversity.

#### Regions
- Adaheimr _north_
- Karnvald _centre, inaccessible following Svartdomr_
- Jadarormr _north east_
