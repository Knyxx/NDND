## Overview
- Sundralith (SOON-drah-lith) — “Sundered Stone” — is a continent of extremes, marked by the lingering scars of Svartdomr. Its landscapes range from frozen northern fjords to temperate southern plains, with rugged mountains, deep valleys, and a devastated central heartland shaping both terrain and culture. The diversity of its lands has fostered isolation among settlements, producing rich but fragmented traditions.

## Points of Interest

- Karnvald _centre, inaccessible following Svartdomr_
- Jadarormr _north east_
