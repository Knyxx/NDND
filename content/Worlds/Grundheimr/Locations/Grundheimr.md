Grundheimr _GRUND-hame_ – “Foundation Home”

#### Overview
The name reflects the Sundralithic belief that the world is the stable foundation of all life and realms, the anchor that holds reality against chaos.

#### Origin Myth
According to lore, the lands off Grundheimr arose from the primordial sea, [[Kargund]], where storming energies and drifting stone fragments collided endlessly. The gods shaped the first stones, binding them into a stable world. Some tales add that the [[World-Tree]]’s roots anchored the land, while the first breath of wind and sky awakened seas, rivers, and skies.

Grundheimr is both home and anchor — the single stable world from which all life, culture, and realms flow. Its creation is remembered as a triumph over the raw, untamed forces of [[Kargund]].
