## Description
- A small fortified settlement in the [[Jade Peaks]] serving as a safe rest for trade caravans, traveling groups, and visiting clans.
- Perched on a strategic plateau at the edge of forested hills and river valleys, it is enclosed by a wooden palisade and a cleared perimeter.
- The village is often shrouded in mist, lending an air of vigilance and quiet tension.
- Its open mustering area, market, and [[Merchant Guild]] office make it a hub of trade, while the keep and watch maintain defence against bandits, predators, and the mysterious forces of the mountains.
- Over decades it has grown into a modest artisan and trading community, balancing the needs of its permanent residents with those of travellers and semi-permanent clan visitors.

## Structures
- Family Homes / Cottages — ~20–25 small timber homes, some with gardens.
- Shared / Multi-family Homes — Houses artisans or extended families.
- Workshops — Blacksmith, carpentry, jade-carving, weaving/pottery.
- Inn / Tavern — Lodging and meals for travellers, mercenaries, and visiting clans.
- Cookhouse / Bakery / Brewery — Food production for residents and guests.
- Granaries / Storage — Crops, dried goods, and trade reserves.
- Barns / Stables — Livestock and pack animals.
- Gardens & Herb Plots — Vegetables, herbs, and medicinal plants.
- Training / Drill Yard — Open area for guard drills and clan exercises.

## Population & Roles
- Permanent Residents: ~100
- Keep & Defence: 15–20 guards and dedicated watch; patrol palisade, gates, and perimeter.
- Administration: 1–2 officials; oversee village affairs, trade records, and security.
- Crafts & Trade: 3–5 artisans; blacksmiths, carpenters, jade carvers, weavers.
- Agriculture & Food: 20–25 farmers and livestock tenders; maintain crops and herds.
- Merchants & Market Support: 10–12 permanent stall operators and shopkeepers; guild office present.
- Services & Hospitality: 5–8 innkeepers, cooks, bakers, brewers.
- Healers & Specialists: 2–3 herbalists, midwives, or ritual guides.
- Laborers & Miscellaneous: 5–8 general workers; construction, clearing, maintenance, palisade upkeep.
- Travelers, Clans & Mercenaries (temporary/semi-permanent): Eastern mustering area and cleared perimeter accommodate 30–60 outsiders, including visiting clans with semi-permanent structures.
