Kraghnok Mine (_KRAGH-nok_) — “Crag Maw”

Location
- [[Adaheimr]] > [[Varkundr]] > [[Crossroads Encampment]]

Notable
- Was hoped to connect to the 'never-ending-veins' of [[Karnvok Mine]] located to the south.
- Produces iron, copper, silver, and rare metals.
- Oldest and largest mine in the range.
- Small settlement at the entrance founded to supports workers and travellers.
- Major veins mined, only small deposits found now
- Has primarily become a fortified storage/shelter
