## Names & Titles
- Tharion Veyrath
- Ash King
- Lord of Rest
- Also remembered in song and ritual as _The Silent Guardian of the Fallen_

## Description
Tharion Veyrath is remembered as a stout Dwarf of imposing presence, clad in darkened armour etched with ash-white runes. Legends say his footsteps carried the weight of the dead he guided, and his hand alone could still [[Restless]] spirits. He walked among battlefields strewn with the dead, wielding divine power alongside his sword to protect the balance between life and death. Artistic depictions often show him with a symbolic holy focus, hinting at his divine connection.

## Information
- Credited with laying countless undead to rest during [[Svartdomr]].
- Founder of the [[Ashwardens]].
- His divine abilities are hinted at in stories: power to heal, shield, and quell unrest, though the true extent of his magic is lost to legend.
- Tharion’s name is invoked during funerals or rituals meant to calm spirits; sacred grounds and burial sites still echo with his mythic influence.

## Connections
- One of the [[Five Kings of Svatdomr]], united to repel the Gargantuan Warps:
	- [[Paladin King Ada]] – his valor complemented Tharion’s solemn guardianship.
	- King Vaelor – collaborated to restore lands corrupted by war and guide survivors.
	- King Sareth – provided magical and tactical tools for battlefield strategy.
	- Queen Lyssara – foresaw where Tharion’s interventions were most needed.
- The [[Ashwardens]], his followers, maintain his legacy and are the living link to his enduring myth.
