## Names & Titles
- Lyria Doss
- [[Merchant Guild]] Scribe of [[Mistfield Clearing]]

## Description
- Half-elf, mid 30's.
- Slender and graceful, with a quiet, reserved demeanor.
- Dark hair usually tied back to keep it out of her face; soft, pointed ears peeking through.
- Wears simple but tidy robes or tunics suitable for writing and clerical work, often with a satchel or pouch of writing tools.
- Eyes are keen and perceptive, always scanning for details.
- Fingers nimble and ink-stained from constant writing.

## Connections
- Works under [[Alvar Kestrel]] at [[Mistfield Clearing]].
- Knows trusted guild couriers and local contacts.

## Personality and Behaviour

- Detail-oriented and methodical, rarely rushed.
- Observant and discreet; notices small inconsistencies or suspicious behaviour.
- Loyal to the guild and diligent in protecting sensitive information.
- Polite but reserved; prefers written communication over confrontation.

Here’s simple advice for playing Lyria Doss at the table:

- Voice & Demeanour: Soft-spoken, careful, precise. Speaks politely but rarely raises her voice.
- Approach: Prefers written communication or formal agreements; avoids confrontation.
- Tactics: Observes first, acts second. Uses records, documentation, and connections to guide decisions or influence outcomes.
- Interactions: Welcomes visitors politely, but is cautious with strangers. Subtlety and discretion are key—she shares information only when appropriate.
- Personality Hook: Loyal and diligent; sees her work as essential to keeping the guild—and Njordsgarde—safe. She respects Alvar and follows his lead.

Keep it simple: she’s a careful, reliable, and discreet bureaucrat, perfect for providing information, contracts, or subtle guidance without taking the spotlight.

Roleplay Lines
- “Please fill out these forms carefully; the guild requires accuracy above all else.”
- “I will ensure your documents reach Njordsgarde safely. Trust in the guild’s channels.”
- “I notice small discrepancies in your records. Would you care to explain?”
- “The guild cannot authorize this without proper approval. I can send a request if you wish.”
- “It is best we handle this quietly; some matters are not for public knowledge.”

Behaviours / Mannerisms
- Constantly adjusts her quill or organizes papers while speaking.
- Glances over documents or ledgers before answering questions, even in conversation.

- Keeps a small stack of ledgers and writing tools always within reach.

Roleplay Tips
- Let her act as a gatekeeper of information; she rarely gives full answers unless trust is established.
