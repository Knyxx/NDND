## Appearance  
- Young, lean, restless Wood Elf with quick, predatory movements.  
- Panther-style shadow tattoos, darker and bolder than elders.  
- Clan mark (three claw-like strokes fading into a swirl) on inner left forearm.  
- Lightweight leather armour, muted forest tones, hood or scarf.  
- Moves silently, blends into mist and shadows, often unseen.
