## Description
- The Iron Talons are a tight-knit, ambitious adventuring party hoping to grow into a full-fledged mercenary company.
- They operate competitively, eager to make a name for themselves, and will stop at little to secure a contract or reward.

##Encounter Notes
- Tactics as a Unit:
	- Kaelin sneaks around to steal the objective or create distractions.
	- Tharok anchors the front, defending Kaelin and Selara.
	- Selara shapes the battlefield with Wild Shape or spells, slowing PCs and summoning animals.
	- Ravik hits multiple targets at range, softening your party before they reach the objective.
- Objective: Beat the PCs to the reward, not necessarily kill them. They may fight, negotiate, or retreat depending on the situation.
- Optional Twist: If PCs win decisively, Kaelin may escape with a small piece of loot or intel, giving a narrative hook for future encounters with the Iron Talons as a growing rival mercenary company.
