## Description

- A small Wood Elf clan living deep within the mist-heavy forests of the [[Jade Peaks]].
- Proven members bear the clan mark—three claw-like strokes fading into a swirl—tattooed on the inner left forearm, as is tradition among all [[Jade Peaks]] clans.
- Their tattoos favour panther-like patterns: dark, flowing shapes and broken shadow markings meant to vanish into fog and low light.
- They are renowned hunters and trackers, respected even among other clans for unmatched stealth and concealment.
- Their survival depends on silence, patience, and intimate knowledge of the land, leaving little trace beyond hidden trails and vanishing footprints.
- Outsiders rarely meet them face-to-face, and most encounters begin with the uneasy sense of being watched.

## Points of Interest

- Hidden Clan Shelters: Small, camouflaged clearings, elevated platforms, and treehouses, offering both safety and vantage points.
- Misty Trails: Subtle, carefully marked paths known only to clan members for travel, hunting, and evasion.

## Connections

- Other [[Jade Peaks]] Clans: Respected, but viewed with suspicion due to the Miststalkers’ secrecy.
- Caravans & Outsiders: Rarely tolerated unless guided, invited, or paying tribute.
- Local Settlements: Minimal contact, though rumours persist of quiet trade in herbs, pelts, and information.
