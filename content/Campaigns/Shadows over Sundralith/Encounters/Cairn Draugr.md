Encounter | 6×L3 Hard

#### Setting
The PCs are fighting in the main chamber of an ancient burial cairn. The air is cold and stale, stone slabs are cracked, and bones litter the floor. The noise disturbs a Draugr resting in a side chamber, who rises and animates four nearby skeletons. A faint greenish glow pulses from the Draugr’s tomb, and mournful moans echo through the corridors.

#### Enemies
| Enemy    | Number | HP  | AC  | Attack                                           | XP  |
| -------- | ------ | --- | --- | ------------------------------------------------ | --- |
| Draugr   | 1      | 90  | 15  | Longsword +5 (1d8+3); Frightful Moan (DC 13 Wis) | 700 |
| Skeleton | 4      | 15  | 13  | Shortsword +3 (1d6+1)                            | 100 |

#### Tactics
- Draugr is disturbed by noise in the main chamber. On its turn, it rises from its side chamber and animates four skeletons.
- Draugr and skeletons move into the main chamber to engage the PCs.
- Draugr uses Frightful Moan at the start of combat if multiple PCs are within range.
- Engages in melee with Longsword +5, targeting the closest or weakest PCs.
- Skeletons use numbers to block movement and harry PCs.
- If Draugr drops below half HP, it may retreat behind debris or back into the side chamber, attacking from cover or using hit-and-run tactics.

#### XP Calculation:
Total Monster XP = 700 + (4 × 100) = 1,100
Number of monsters = 5 → Multiplier = 2.0 (per DMG p.82)
Adjusted XP: 1,100 × 2 = 2,200 → Hard for 6 level 3 PCs
