#### Setting
The party comes across a pale, humming spirit — Goldi — unaware she is dead.
When she realises the truth, she unleashes a powerful scream that awakens three undead bears in the frozen cave.

#### Goldi’s Initial Scream (Opening Round Only)
Effect: All creatures within 40 ft hear Goldi’s first, explosive wail.
Save: CON save DC 15
On Fail:
- Take 3d6 psychic damage
- Frightened of Goldi until the end of your next turn
- Disadvantage on your next attempt to attack (even after the fear ends)
On Success:
- Take half damage, not frightened.
This scream happens once, then she reverts to her weaker round-to-round wail.

#### Tactics
- Goldi moves constantly, weaving through terrain and using stronger psychic pressure than before.
- Empowering Wail+ makes the bears extremely hard to permanently kill—focus fire is required.
- Papa Bear (Elite Tank/Striker):
	- Targets the highest-damage PC
	- Advantage on saves vs being shoved or knocked prone
	- Hits brutally hard
- Mama Bear (Elite Controller):
	- Attempts to restrain or hold down PCs with Icy Shackles
	- Grants advantage to Papa and Baby when restraining
- Baby Bear (Savage Flanker):
	- Pack Tactics ensures consistent hits
	- Prioritises casters, archers, or anyone isolated
- Bears fight until completely destroyed after revival is no longer possible.
- Encounter ends when bears fall and Goldi is soothed.
