## Setting
A concealed bandit supply cache hidden in a ruin.
The crumbling walls form rough cover.
The bandits are focused on the job, not on security.

## Tactics
- If the party sneaks in quietly, they can ambush and eliminate a scout before combat begins.
- Scout on lookout - distracted, watching the work
- Thugs
	- distracted, complaining lounge near the fire and rush to engage once an alarm is raised
	- one is peeing away from the others - who the players hear if passing the perception check
- Captain
	- Sleeping behind the scout
	- Sneak attack on party if wakes before being found
