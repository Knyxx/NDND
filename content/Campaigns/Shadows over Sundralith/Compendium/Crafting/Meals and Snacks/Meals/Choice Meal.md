## Description
- General buff combo item.

## Properties
- Crafting and Usage modified by your Proficiency with Cook's Utensils.

## Usage
- Time to Consume: 15 minutes.

## Effects
- Provides Advantage on your next Saving Throw.
- Gain 2d8 + (Proficiency Bonus x 2) temporary hit points.
- Gain +1 bonus to AC for 6 hours.
- Spoils in 6 hours if not consumed.
