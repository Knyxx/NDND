## SETUP
Ambushing bandits in the midst of moving a stockpile of resources the party returns [[Tor Emberdelve|Tor]] to the [[Crossroads Encampment]] only to find an injured [[Skald]] speaking of a bandit attack, having spirited the children away while [[Vargas]] and [[Moko]] bravely fought them off.

Quickly healing [[Skald]] and finding the others had yet to return the party immediately set out into the dwindling light in search, passing other guards both living and dead before lucking out on a blood trail leading them to a heavily wounded [[Vargas]] and a terrified [[Moko]].

Stabilising [[Vargas]] they decide - wisely - to rest further up the hill in an abandoned, ruined house, before returning down the hill and back to [[Crossroads Encampment]] the next day to the camps delight.
