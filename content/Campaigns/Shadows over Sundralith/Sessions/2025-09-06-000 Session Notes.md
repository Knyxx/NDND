#### GOAL
- To craft an immersive story together, in a safe and fun way for all — where death is real, danger is constant, and every choice can shape fate or bring failure

#### PRACTICES
- Absences: group vote
- Communication: PC to PC preferred
- Etiquette: Camera on (if possible), avoid interruptions, raise hand
- Recaps: No

#### HOUSE RULES
- System: D&D 5E (2024) + Rule of Cool
- Balance: Anything PCs can do, so can NPCs
- New PCs: Start at Level 3, 200G, common gear
- Stats & HP: Rolled with party (fun, not strict). HP rolled each level.
	- Recommended buy: Explorer's Backpack 10G
	- Level up health rolls for all levels, chose to accept and level or not
- Weight: Coins, ammo, and carry limits enforced
- Loot & Gold: Split evenly, key items direct to character
- Critical Success/Fail on check guarantees success/fail - pass fail - no indicate
- PvP (outside combat): Only with mutual agreement
- Combat: Prep: 2m, Turn: 60s
	- Flanking: 2 allies within 5ft. you have advantage
	- Nat20 = full weapon damage + roll weapon damage + mods
	- Nat1 = consequence
- Death Saves: Player muted, DM rolls (hidden), tells player
	- Nat20: immediate revive with 1 health
	- Success: survive, 0 health, unconscious
	- Nat1: two fails
	- 3 Fails: see character creation

#### WORLD BUILDING
- Limits:
	- Off-screen = mental distress
	- Avoid = sexual assault, drugging
- Tone: Heroic but gritty; combat · roleplay · exploration · mystery.
- Interests: everything (combat, exploration, mystery, roleplay)
- Tone: Challenging but fair: death is real, and every player choice shapes the threats faced and the consequences earned
- Requests:
	- Gelatinous Cube
	- Seymour Guado, Final Fantasy 10 - Boss Fight

- Connections: PC to PC, PC to world (story, goals, etc.)
