## SETUP

The [[Crossroads Encampment]] was alive with celebration as the party returned. Lanterns swayed gently in the evening breeze, casting flickering light over the village paths, while the scent of roasting meat and sweet pastries drifted from the small stalls. Fiddles and drums set a lively rhythm, punctuated by the laughter of children and the clinking of mugs, and the villagers’ faces shone with relief and joy. The miraculous return and healing of several beloved figures had lifted spirits, and the honored dead had been laid to rest with quiet dignity—now the festival served as both remembrance and renewal.

[[Gar-ruk]], weary from long roads and troubling revelations, wasted no time. He moved straight to the nearest barrel, lifting it to his lips with a practiced urgency. The rest of the party, carried along by camaraderie and the infectious cheer of the encampment, followed his example, joining the revelry with laughter and a little chaos. [[Elara]], steadfast as ever, remained apart, watching with a mixture of amusement and concern as her companions drank deeply. When the dawn finally arrived, the party awoke scattered across the encampment, heads heavy, spirits muddled, and memories of the night blurred but warm in the heart.

Once they had gathered their wits, they sought out [[Tor Emberdelve|Tor]], who welcomed them with quiet solemnity. He spoke of the [[Ember Order]], founded by his ancestor, of bonds forged with [[Durough Oxfell|Durough]], and of knowledge long hidden—secrets tucked within the tomb of [[Paladin King Ada]], waiting for the right hands to uncover them. Each revelation deepened the party’s understanding of the world they had returned to, and of the threats that now crept closer.

Amid the morning bustle, another figure caught their attention: a [[Robust Guard Bot]], a Warforged reminiscent of [[RGB]], bearing a Token of [[Selûne]] on a sash. A brief meeting with [[Tom Stormbrand|Tom]] clarified its purpose, and the party left the encampment with new direction and resolve, their hearts set on the challenges ahead.

Driven by the desire to grow stronger against the rising dangers, they turned northward. Days passed under sun and wind, the landscape shifting around them. At night, when they made camp, the tang of salt on the breeze carried a quiet warning, and the distant rhythm of hooves to the south reminded them that they were no longer alone on these roads. The world was vast, and it was waiting.
