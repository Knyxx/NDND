A year has passed since [[Elderrend]] shook Sundralith to its foundations. The land slowly heals: forests push through ash-strewn valleys, rivers run clearer, and scattered settlements have begun to thrive. Amid this fragile recovery, hope and resilience pulse through the survivors, and the people dare to dream of stability once more.

Yet beneath this growth, the land itself seems uneasy. Strange phenomena stir in the wilds, ancient ruins pulse with arcane energy, and rumors of unusual creatures circulate among traders and scouts. Whispers speak of forces long forgotten, of secrets buried beneath mountains, forests, and rivers—secrets that demand action.

Into this uncertain world comes a party of mismatched individuals, drawn together by circumstance, ambition, or fate. Each carries their own motives, skills, and agendas, but all must navigate a land where danger grows quietly in the shadows, where allies may hide their true intentions, and where mysteries beckon with promises of power—or peril.

In Shadows Over Sundralith, discovery and danger walk hand in hand. The choices of today may shape the fate of tomorrow, and only those bold enough to act—and cautious enough to watch the hidden threads—will survive and uncover the true story behind the land’s rising shadows.
