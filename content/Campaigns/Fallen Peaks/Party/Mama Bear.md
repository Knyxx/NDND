Bethany Ruith was bought up in the forest on the outskirts of the small village of ( TBD ).
Her father (Allanon) was a gentle human druid who tended the forest. Her mother (Carrie) was a mercenary, not popular with the local village, her rather terse attitude did not help rumors of "something wrong with her". 
Bethany is the second of four children. She has an older sister, Moonbeam, and two younger brothers, Cecil and Trifa.
Moonbeam resented the rather sheltered life and left for the city as soon as she came of age. She also despised her name and insisted on mering called Meredith.
Her mother was often away on one adventure or another, leaving the children in the care of her father. Moonbeam would spend a lot her tie in the village  while (father) would have duties that would take him into the forest for long hours. Bethany would happily look after her younger brother. 
Bethany first found out she shared some of her mothers condition when she found her brothers being bullied in village by some larger children. Her features had often been considered peculiar, but these were accentuated hair and bearlike features become pronounced as she came to her brothers rescue. It was at this point that the name "Mamma Bear" came about.
Her father instructed Bethany in druid craft, or at least tried to. While she loved nature and was interested , there were aspects of the training she was not able to grasp. While learning his craft she would come into contact with some of the fey in the forest. She knew some in the village had fey ancestry but had never heard the elven language spoken. She would sometimes go off alone into the forest to meet them, getting friendly with at least one.
After her first shifting, her mother started training her in more combat related skills when she was home. Bethany had seen her mother practice before but never appreciated how much work was put into it. Bethany felt her mother was a hard task master at first, but as her skill and confidence grew she found an aptitude and joy in the practice. She soon started incorporating her fury and shifting into her routines.
At 18 her mother invited her to come on a job with her guarding a caravan. This was her first taste of real combat as the caravan was attacked by bandits a few days into its travel. Bethany initially hung back in the fight but charged in to defend a wagon driver that was being rush by two of the bandits. After the battle was over she was surprised to find three bandit bodies around her, thinking she had only engaged two. It wasn't until her mother came over to retrieve her throwing axe out of the back of one of them that she realised she wasn't the only Mamma bear in the family.
She was glad to return home a regale her brothers of her tales of her first outing. But at the same time home felt smaller than she remembered. She realised how sheltered her life had been, understood the wandering of her mother more, and even maybe the why her sister had wanted to leave.
On another caravan guarding trip, a village they passed through was raided by hobgoblins slavers. At one point she was struck down and carried as in the raid, only to wake up chained in a raiding camp miles away. The hobgoblins travelled for days with their catch of prisoners. Eventually they arrived at a fortified mine camp where she was put to work. She was put to work in the mines for a week before a collapse in the mine sealed her and anther prisoner off from the rest of the work party. Believing they were dead, the hobgoblins directed the work force to start in another area of the mine rather than risk loosing any others. The collapse and near a fissure that lead to a natural cavern system. Bethany and the other prisoner managed to find an opening a day latter and escaped. Finding their way back to the village that was raided she was reunited with her mother. It took a month of gathering resources and hiring mercenaries, funded by themselves and some of the families of the prisoners, before they were able to launch a rescue mission to the hobgoblin camp. Bethany showed the way tot e mines and the troop waiting until a raiding party left before launching their attack. While the initial attack involved stealth and taking the camp was successful, the raiders had turned back for some reason and they company was forced to defend the encampment themselves. This was a harder fight but the surviving hobgoblins were driven off . Both Bethany and her mother sustained serious wounds in that battle that are left scars to this day.
Bethany and her mother decided to keep most of the details of the trip from the rest of the family as to not alarm her father.
Both of her brothers ended up getting work on farms or apprenticeships as they came of age and left home.
Around that time her mother retired from the mercenary life and stayed at home.
Without her brothers to look after she felt a bit empty. Protecting people and action was something that she yearned for. So with a supplies from her father and a sharpening stone from her mother she set out to find something to fulfil her life.
