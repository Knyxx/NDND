Five days ago, an inspection party vanished somewhere in the [[Jade Peaks]], a wild and mist-shrouded mountain range that rises in endless green ridges before sharpening into towering, cloud-wrapped peaks. The party was sent by a high-ranking official of the [[Merchant Guild]] to inspect holdings in the region. No word has come back.

Now, a crowd of locals, mercenaries, and adventurers gathers in [[Mistfield Clearing]]. Tents and stalls ring the open space. Smoke from cookfires drifts into the low-lying fog. The air smells of damp pine, salted meat, and something faintly metallic, carried on the cold mountain breeze.

Whispers ripple through the crowd—rumours, warnings, fear. The mist moves like slow breath through the clearing, and somewhere beyond the tree line lie abandoned mines, deep hollows, and unseen dangers.

You don’t know each other yet. You’ve come with little more than your wits, your weapons, and your courage.

The [[Merchant Guild]] offers reward and supplies to anyone willing to venture into the Peaks to find the missing party. But the mountains are unpredictable, the fog thick, and the forested ridges hide more than lost travellers.

Will you step forward into the mist? Will you uncover what happened to those who vanished? Or will the [[Jade Peaks]] claim more than just the lost?
