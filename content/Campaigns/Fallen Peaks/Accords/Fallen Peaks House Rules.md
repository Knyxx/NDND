## Overview
### System
- Learn by playing.
- Rules: D&D 5.5e (2024) + Rule of Cool.
- World: Choice and consequence. PCs and NPCs follow the same rules.

### Tone & Style
- Tone: Varies, often dark.
- Stories: Wide range.
- Requests: Political Murder Mystery.
- Lethality: Character death is possible.

### Table Etiquette
- PvP outside combat is permitted.
- Share the spotlight, avoid interrupting/talking over others.
- Minimise distractions where possible.

### Sessions
- Frequency: Fortnightly (scheduled via group chat).
- Length: ~3 hours.
- Keep interruptions minimal.
- Use a clear signal to participate (raise hand, message, etc.).

---
## Characters

### New Characters
- Starting Level: 1.
- Starting Gear: Standard.
- Advancement: Experience (XP).
- Ability Scores: Rolled as a group.
- Hit Points: Rolled as a group. Max roll for Level 1.
- Encumbrance: Carry weight tracked.
- Currency: Currency weight not tracked.
- Ammunition: Tracked when relevant (e.g. dungeons).

---
## Gameplay

### Ability Checks & Difficulty
- Success: Meet or exceed the DC.
- Natural 20: Automatic success.
- Natural 1: Automatic failure.
- Secret Rolls: The DM may roll in secret at a player’s request.

### Exhaustion
- Dropping to 0 HP immediately causes 1 level of Exhaustion (in addition to other effects).

### Play Style
- The world is for the characters; the game is for the players.
- No single “right” way to play.
- Act freely, but don’t sabotage the party or dominate the table.
- Stay focused during active scenes.
- Jokes and reactions are fine—return to the scene after.

---
## Combat

### Attack Roll
- Critical Hit (Nat 20): Automatic Hit. Maximum damage + normal roll + modifiers.
- Critical Miss (Nat 1): Automatic Miss. DM may apply an extra consequence.

### Experience (XP)
- XP split evenly among participating allies.
- NPC allies who meaningfully contribute (alive or dead) share in XP.
