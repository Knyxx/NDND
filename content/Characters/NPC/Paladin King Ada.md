NPC | [[Human]] | [[Paladin]] ([[Oath of Justice]])
![[Paladin King Ada.jpg]]

Born in the northern reaches of [[Sundralith]], he joined the [[Brotherhood]] at a young age to train and serve his people.

Among the group that fought off the first of the [[Great Beasts]] and later became one of the [[Five Kings]] responsible for ending [[Svartdomr]].

After his deeds, he was laid to rest in [[Adahowe]] by his four closest friends and generals: [[Kaelen of the Mists]], [[Eryndor Frostmantle]], [[Grusk Oxfell]], and [[Dain Emberdelve]].
