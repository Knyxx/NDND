#### Story
- A proud [[Orc]] of the [[Oxfell Clan]], [[Gromash Oxfell]] was raised in the [[Oxfell Clanhold]] beside his brother [[Brukkar Oxfell]], who would later rise as clan head. When the [[Ember Order]], guardians of the ancient keys, came with warnings of growing [[Chaos Energy]] and spreading [[Dimensional Breach#Fracture|fractures]], the clan rejected their counsel. Unwilling to gamble with his family’s lives, Gromash left the Clanhold, taking them—and the hidden [[Storm Disk]]—to seek the other key guardians himself.
- He turned to a [[mercenary group]] he had once fought beside, hoping for sanctuary, but before reaching safety they were ambushed. His wife [[Hedra Oxfell|Hedra]] fell defending their infant grandson [[Gar-ruk Oxfell|Gar-ruk]], a loss that left Gromash hollow with grief yet steadfast in purpose. To protect what remained, he placed his son [[Oxfell, Brukk|Brukk]], Brukk’s wife [[Thyra Oxfell|Thyra]], and baby Gar-ruk in a secluded [[small farm]]. With his eldest grandson [[Durough Oxfell|Durough]], already grown and battle-ready, he rejoined the mercenaries in their search for the other guardians.
- Rumors led them toward the [[Jade Peaks]], where Gromash scouted ahead alone. Captured, he was sentenced not for crime but for dangerous knowledge, condemned to labor in the depths of [[Skywatch Fortress]] for years. There, he forged an unlikely bond with [[Zamos|Zamos, the Wayward]], disgraced son of the [[Cloudrunner Clan]]. Gromash shared tales of home and hardship, though never his true name, fearing misfortune might hunt his bloodline.
- When Zamos resolved to abandon his past and claim a new future, he refused to leave the old orc behind. He released Gromash, allowing him to escape, but the escape faltered when [[Gaia Mistwalker|Gaia]], Zamos’s mother, confronted Gromash. At that same moment, [[Durough Oxfell|Durough]], who had tracked his grandfather to the fortress and snuck inside to free him during an assault he planned, wounded Gaia, unaware of her identity.
- Stricken that his grandson had harmed the woman who had shown him only kindness, Gromash rushed to her side—only to see the relic he had sought to safeguard, the [[Fire Disk]], hanging from her neck. As battle raged, he tore it free and dragged Durough from the fortress, torn between blood, duty, and ruin.
- Freed at last, he discovered much had changed during his long imprisonment. Seeking his family, he despaired as one by one he learned of their fates. Entrusting the [[Fire Disk]] and its secrets to his last remaining kin, Durough, he was horrified to uncover that the [[mercenary group]] had long been part of the [[Ember Order]]—those whose warnings had set the tragic chain of events in motion. Unable to convince Durough of their treachery, and unwilling to raise his hand against him, Gromash left in sorrow.
- He returned to [[Adaheimr]], where only a handful of old hunting dogs scavenged his abandoned home. There he settled in quiet exile, a once-proud guardian bound by grief and regret, yet resolute in purpose. Even in solitude, he remained vigilant, silently guarding the hidden [[Storm Disk]], the relic he had sworn to protect, and holding on to the fading hope that his bloodline might yet endure.
