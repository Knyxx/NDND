NPC | [[Winter Eladrin]] | [[Ranger]] ([[Gloom Stalker]])
![[Eryndor Frostmantle.jpg]]

Eryndor, a Winter [[Eladrin]] of the high glacial reaches, held the frozen passes with only his bow, striking down any barbarian foolish enough to descend from the north. His presence was as timeless as the snows he guarded, guiding and protecting the peaks with a fey’s patient watch.

Drawn from his solitude by [[Paladin King Ada]] to aid in ending [[Svartdomr]], Eryndor became his first general and was later entrusted with the seal [[Ice Disk]].
