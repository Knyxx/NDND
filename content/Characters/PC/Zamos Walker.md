#### Story
- Born [[Zamos Walker|Amos, the Deliverer]], son of [[Auros Cloudrunner]] and [[Gaia Mistwalker]].
- Raised in [[Skywatch Fortress]] atop [[Mount Dawnveil]], he was trained in sword and spirit, destined to awaken the sacred [[Twinsunder]]. When he failed the rite, he was cast aside, renamed [[Zamos Walker|Zamos, the Wayward]], and shunned by his father—yet his mother continued to guide him in discipline and arms.
- As a blacksmith, [[Zamos Walker|Zamos]] befriended [[Gromash Oxfell|Gromash]], an [[orc]] prisoner whose tales of distant lands stirred his imagination. Moved to free him, [[Zamos Walker|Zamos]] sparked chaos that claimed his mother’s life. Before he could confront [[Gromash Oxfell|Gromash]], [[Skywatch Fortress]] fell to a sudden assault, leaving [[Zamos Walker|Zamos]] its only survivor.
- Vowing that none would suffer for his mercy again, he pursued the [[Ember Order|perpetrators]] of the massacre. His search uncovered records pointing to [[Adaheimr]], leading him to the [[Crossroads Encampment]], where he found not ruin but resilience—survivors rebuilding in the shadow of [[Elderrend]], echoing the spirit of his lost home.
- Now, [[Zamos Walker|Zamos]] walks the line between vengeance and guardianship—defender by day, hunter by night. Each strike bears his mother’s lessons, each step the weight of his fallen clan—until a crashing sound within the encampment draws him toward the next turn in his fate.

Born [[Zamos Walker|Amos, the Deliverer]], son of [[Auros Cloudrunner]] and [[Gaia Mistwalker]].
Raised in [[Skywatch Fortress]] atop [[Mount Dawnveil]], he was trained in sword and spirit, destined to awaken the sacred [[Twinsunder]]. When he failed the rite, he was cast aside, renamed [[Zamos Walker|Zamos, the Wayward]], and shunned by his father—yet his mother continued to guide him in discipline and arms.
As a blacksmith, [[Zamos Walker|Zamos]] befriended [[Gromash Oxfell|Gromash]], an [[orc]] prisoner whose tales of distant lands stirred his imagination. Moved to free him, [[Zamos Walker|Zamos]] sparked chaos that claimed his mother’s life. Before he could confront [[Gromash Oxfell|Gromash]], [[Skywatch Fortress]] fell to a sudden assault, leaving [[Zamos Walker|Zamos]] its only survivor.
Vowing that none would suffer for his mercy again, he pursued the [[Ember Order|perpetrators]] of the massacre. His search uncovered records pointing to [[Adaheimr]], leading him to the [[Crossroads Encampment]], where he found not ruin but resilience—survivors rebuilding in the shadow of [[Elderrend]], echoing the spirit of his lost home.
Now, [[Zamos Walker|Zamos]] walks the line between vengeance and guardianship—defender by day, hunter by night. Each strike bears his mother’s lessons, each step the weight of his fallen clan—until a crashing sound within the encampment draws him toward the next turn in his fate.
