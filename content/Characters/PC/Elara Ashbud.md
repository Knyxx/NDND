#### Story
- [[Elara Ashbud]] was raised among the [[ancient groves]], where trees whispered their secrets through rustling leaves. Known for her quiet kindness and creative spirit rather than boldness, she grew up believing the forest was both her home and protector, and in return swore to defend it. Her mentors taught her to move in silence, to strike unseen when the balance was threatened, and above all to watch with patience rather than haste.
- Yet her duty never hardened her spirit. [[Elara Ashbud|Elara]] cherished the small wonders of the wild—nurturing saplings she believed would one day stand as guardians, tending flowers thought to bring good fortune, and healing wounded animals with the conviction that all life deserved compassion. In the village, she was best loved for her [[firefly lanterns]], their soft glow carrying laughter and old tales into the night and briefly freeing her from her shyness.
- When shadows began to stir at the edges of the forest—beasts straying from the deep wilds, whispers of bandits, rumours of darker things—[[Elara Ashbud|Elara]] realized that tending the groves would no longer be enough. She set out with her bow and a [[Ashbud’s Spirit Lantern|special lantern]], home to a tree spirit that had chosen to accompany her. Together they joined a caravan bound for the [[Crossroads Encampment]] in [[Adaheimr]], where [[Elara Ashbud|Elara]] lent her skills to see the wagons safely through.
- That night, after camp was made, she wandered the outskirts to gain her bearings. In the silver glow of moonlight she spotted a [[Warforged]] working in the dirt, tending a patch of earth with uncanny care. As she watched, her [[Ashbud’s Spirit Lantern|lantern]] stirred—the [[Tree Spirit|spirit]] within growing suddenly restless, its glow pulsing as though it sensed something familiar. Then the construct straightened and bolted into the night. Compelled by both curiosity and the spirit’s strange excitement, [[Elara Ashbud|Elara]] hurried after it . . .

[[Elara Ashbud]] was raised among the [[ancient groves]], where trees whispered their secrets through rustling leaves. Known for her quiet kindness and creative spirit rather than boldness, she grew up believing the forest was both her home and protector, and in return swore to defend it. Her mentors taught her to move in silence, to strike unseen when the balance was threatened, and above all to watch with patience rather than haste.
Yet her duty never hardened her spirit. [[Elara Ashbud|Elara]] cherished the small wonders of the wild—nurturing saplings she believed would one day stand as guardians, tending flowers thought to bring good fortune, and healing wounded animals with the conviction that all life deserved compassion. In the village, she was best loved for her [[firefly lanterns]], their soft glow carrying laughter and old tales into the night and briefly freeing her from her shyness.
When shadows began to stir at the edges of the forest—beasts straying from the deep wilds, whispers of bandits, rumours of darker things—[[Elara Ashbud|Elara]] realized that tending the groves would no longer be enough. She set out with her bow and a [[Ashbud’s Spirit Lantern|special lantern]], home to a tree spirit that had chosen to accompany her. Together they joined a caravan bound for the [[Crossroads Encampment]] in [[Adaheimr]], where [[Elara Ashbud|Elara]] lent her skills to see the wagons safely through.
That night, after camp was made, she wandered the outskirts to gain her bearings. In the silver glow of moonlight she spotted a [[Warforged]] working in the dirt, tending a patch of earth with uncanny care. As she watched, her [[Ashbud’s Spirit Lantern|lantern]] stirred—the [[Tree Spirit|spirit]] within growing suddenly restless, its glow pulsing as though it sensed something familiar. Then the construct straightened and bolted into the night. Compelled by both curiosity and the spirit’s strange excitement, [[Elara Ashbud|Elara]] hurried after it . . .
