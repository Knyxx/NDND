#### Story
- Once a bird with nothing but feathers and a love for shiny things, Maggie was transformed into a human by strange, powerful magic. Her memories of that change are like fragments of a dream: a voice too vast to name, flashes of impossible light, and a lingering sense that she must seek treasure… and something called a dragon.
- Now quick-footed and wide-eyed, Maggie wanders the world with more curiosity than caution. Trinkets vanish into her pockets as naturally as twigs once lined her nest, and trouble follows her not by malice, but by the irresistible pull of discovery. Every coin, jewel, or polished buckle is a little wonder she can hold, a small echo of the world she barely remembers.
- One evening, she overhears gossip of gold and a dragon on the far side of the encampment. The words stir something deep within her, a faint echo of that dreamlike memory. She doesn’t know what a dragon truly is — perhaps a very big bird, or a moving treasure — but the thought of it makes her heart race.
- When night falls, Maggie slips away, shadows and curiosity carrying her forward. She cannot explain why, only that deep inside she feels this treasure matters — more than anything she’s ever chased before.
- Creeping through the camp under cover of darkness, her tiny heart pounding, she follows the whispers to a large, imposing house at the edge of the encampment. Every step is a careful tiptoe, every shadow a potential hiding place.
- Inside, she discovers a vast storage room, shelves piled high with boxes, jars, and mysterious bundles. Glittering things peek from every corner: coins spilling from a tipped-over chest, strings of beads dangling like vines, and small metal trinkets catching the flickering light. Maggie’s eyes widen — it is more treasure than she could have imagined.
- Curious, she begins poking around, her fingers brushing over shiny buttons, glass baubles, and polished orbs. Then she sees it: a great, twelve-foot-wide shiny ball, glowing faintly in the dim light. Something inside it — a shadow shaped like a man — frozen, yet so lifelike.
- Intrigued, Maggie leans closer, craning her neck to see inside. She reaches out, just a little too eagerly… and accidentally knocks the ball. It wobbles, teeters, and rolls straight toward her with a soft clatter.
- Maggie freezes, wide-eyed. She hadn’t meant to — but now the shiny ball is coming right at her. She reaches out instinctively, mind racing: what is inside it, and how can she get out of the way?

Once a bird with nothing but feathers and a love for shiny things, Maggie was transformed into a human by strange, powerful magic. Her memories of that change are like fragments of a dream: a voice too vast to name, flashes of impossible light, and a lingering sense that she must seek treasure… and something called a dragon.
Now quick-footed and wide-eyed, Maggie wanders the world with more curiosity than caution. Trinkets vanish into her pockets as naturally as twigs once lined her nest, and trouble follows her not by malice, but by the irresistible pull of discovery. Every coin, jewel, or polished buckle is a little wonder she can hold, a small echo of the world she barely remembers.
One evening, she overhears gossip of gold and a dragon on the far side of the encampment. The words stir something deep within her, a faint echo of that dreamlike memory. She doesn’t know what a dragon truly is — perhaps a very big bird, or a moving treasure — but the thought of it makes her heart race.
When night falls, Maggie slips away, shadows and curiosity carrying her forward. She cannot explain why, only that deep inside she feels this treasure matters — more than anything she’s ever chased before.
Creeping through the camp under cover of darkness, her tiny heart pounding, she follows the whispers to a large, imposing house at the edge of the encampment. Every step is a careful tiptoe, every shadow a potential hiding place.
Inside, she discovers a vast storage room, shelves piled high with boxes, jars, and mysterious bundles. Glittering things peek from every corner: coins spilling from a tipped-over chest, strings of beads dangling like vines, and small metal trinkets catching the flickering light. Maggie’s eyes widen — it is more treasure than she could have imagined.
Curious, she begins poking around, her fingers brushing over shiny buttons, glass baubles, and polished orbs. Then she sees it: a great, twelve-foot-wide shiny ball, glowing faintly in the dim light. Something inside it — a shadow shaped like a man — frozen, yet so lifelike.
Intrigued, Maggie leans closer, craning her neck to see inside. She reaches out, just a little too eagerly… and accidentally knocks the ball. It wobbles, teeters, and rolls straight toward her with a soft clatter.
Maggie freezes, wide-eyed. She hadn’t meant to — but now the shiny ball is coming right at her. She reaches out instinctively, mind racing: what is inside it, and how can she get out of the way?
