#### Story
- [[Gar-ruk Oxfell]] was born in 969 A.D., the son of [[Brukk Oxfell|Brukk]] and [[Thyra Oxfell|Thyra]], and younger brother to [[Durough Oxfell|Durough]].
- His childhood on a [[small farm]] was quiet, shaped by the unwavering strength of his father and the gentle guidance of his mother. With his brother away serving as a soldier, [[Gar-ruk Oxfell|Gar-ruk]] inherited [[Durough Oxfell|Durough]]’s [[Oxfell Clan Studded Leather|old leather armour]], a small link to the sibling he never knew.
- Tragedy struck early. At twelve, a pack of rabid wolves attacked their farm. His father died defending him and his mother, who fled with [[Gar-ruk Oxfell|Gar-ruk]] to a nearby village. Two years later, [[Thyra Oxfell|Thyra]] was claimed by a sudden, violent wasting sickness. Alone and destitute, [[Gar-ruk Oxfell|Gar-ruk]] became a wayfarer, learning to survive through skill, cunning, and steel.

Hardened by loss, [[Gar-ruk Oxfell|Gar-ruk]] returned to the [[Crossroads Encampment]], determined to uncover the shadowy forces that had destroyed his family—and to discover the fate of his brother.

Two years later, [[Thyra Oxfell|Thyra]] was claimed by a sudden, violent wasting sickness.
